## 2026-05-24 — Multi-role bundle-first MQAR (pre-gate 1, Report 067)

**Session shape.** Single-coherent-commit landed for the Report 065/066 chain. Codex pre-Phase-5'-commit gate 1 (test whether `Σ bind(role, filler)` multi-role bundles survive cleanup, not just single (k, v) pairs) executed on the existing harness via a new `bundle_first_multirole_multiscene_key_only` strategy. Production sweep: D=4096, n_queries=512, 3 seeds, K_roles ∈ {2, 4, 8}, N ∈ {16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512}, wall time 14:51 on MPS, 27,648 trials.

**Strategy design.** N scenes, each a K-role bundle: `bundle_n = normalize(Σ bind(r_j, content[filler_n_j]))`. Stored as one MHN pattern per scene; content codebook (size C=1024) stored separately for cleanup. Query: pick (n, j_known, j_query) where j_query ≠ j_known. Cue = `normalize(bind(r_{j_known}, content[filler_n_{j_known}]))` — one known (role, filler) pair from the scene. **Stage 1:** scene-MHN settles on cue → recovers the scene bundle. **Stage 2:** algebraic `unbind(scene_state, r_{j_query})` → noisy estimate of `content[filler_n_{j_query}]`. **Stage 3:** content-MHN cleanup over C=1024 codebook → top-1.

**Diagnostics.** Two basin-retrieval signals per query: `scene_top_index_hits` (stage 1 MHN argmax matches the cued scene) and `content_top_index_hits` (stage 3 MHN argmax matches the queried filler). These were the missing diagnostics that caused the Report 066 GHRR walk-back; their inclusion here pre-empts the algebraic-decode confound entirely.

|---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 16  | 0.984 | 1.000 | 0.995 | 1536/1536 | 1536/1536 |
| 64  | 0.979 | 0.971 | 0.969 | 1491/1536 | 1491/1536 |
| 128 | 0.935 | 0.938 | 0.930 | 1441/1536 | 1441/1536 |
| 256 | 0.889 | 0.888 | 0.877 | 1364/1536 | 1364/1536 |
| 512 | 0.784 | 0.792 | 0.799 | 1216/1536 | 1216/1536 |

**Three findings.**
- **F-1 — Multi-role bundle-first passes the gate.** Top-1 stays ≥88% to N=256 across all K_roles. At N=512 (where single-role bundle_first dropped to 61.5%) multi-role retains 78-80%. `Σ bind(role, filler)` survives cleanup at MQAR diagnostic scale.
- **F-2 — Scene-MHN is the sole bottleneck.** Of 54 (K, N, seed) cells × 1536 trials each, `scene_tix == content_tix` in 53 cells and differs by 1 in 1 cell. Cleanup is essentially deterministic given correct scene identification. This rules out the codex-flagged algebraic-decode confound that broke the original Report 066 GHRR claim: top-1 cannot be inflated when both stage-1 and stage-3 MHN argmax track the same accuracy curve.
- **F-3 — K_roles ∈ {2, 4, 8} barely affects the curve.** Cross-K variance per N is ~1% absolute, comparable to cross-seed variance. Per-scene bundle complexity is not the binding constraint in this regime. **Structural insight.** Single-role bundle_first stored N pairs in one bundle and cleaned up over a value codebook of size N. Multi-role bundle-first stores N small bundles (K pairs each) as separate MHN patterns and unbinds from a *small* K-pair bundle. The cleanup cost shifts from "Plate bundle capacity at total-pairs=N" to "MHN capacity at N stored patterns + Plate capacity at K=O(1) per pattern." As N grows, this is the more scalable factorization — and the production numbers confirm it (multi-role > single-role at N=512).

**Why the codex pre-commit gate was the right call.** Without F-2 specifically, the algebraic-decode confound risk from Report 066 would have persisted into Phase 5' design. The scene_tix vs content_tix split makes that risk concretely auditable — and the result (essentially 1:1) is the clearest possible "this rescue is real, not a measurement artifact" signal.
