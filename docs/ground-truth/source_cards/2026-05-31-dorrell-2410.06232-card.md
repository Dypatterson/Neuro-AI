- **Claim:** Analytical characterization of the OPTIMA of a **linear** autoencoder with a **nonnegativity constraint on the latent** and an energy-efficient objective. Setup (§2.1, Eq. 2): `z = W_in·s + b_in`, `s = W_out·z + b_out`, `z ≥ 0`; minimize `⟨||z||₂²⟩_{p(s)} + λ(||W_out||_F² + ||W_in||_F²)` — mean activity energy plus an L2/Frobenius weight penalty, subject to `z≥0`. The paper names the ML analogues verbatim: "**ReLU activation functions and weight decay, respectively**." **Theorem 2.1** (verbatim): "At the minima of this problem, the representation modularises, i.e. each row of W_in has at most one non-zero entry, **iff** [a stated inequality on the source-support extremes + pairwise correlations] is satisfied for all w∈ℝ^{d_s}." **Theorem 2.2** (verbatim): define `E = {y : yᵀF⁻¹y = 1}`; "the representation modularises **iff E lies within the convex hull of the datapoints**." **Corollary 2.3** (verbatim): "The optimal representation modularises **if all sources are pairwise extreme-point independent**" (range/extreme-point independence of the SUPPORT is sufficient). The load-bearing surprise: the condition is **range independence, NOT statistical independence** (Fig 1c: a statistical-interdependence measure does not predict modularity). **λ-independence** (verbatim, §2.3): "**Remarkably, they do not depend on the energy tradeoff parameter λ.**" So modularity is a property of the optimum under nonnegativity + support geometry — the penalty weight does not tune it. This is **representation factorization at the optimum, NOT held-out prediction** (no generalization eval anywhere). Analytic backbone: Appendix B "Positive Linear Autoencoder Theory" (Thms B.1–B.3, Cor B.4), derived under `z≥0` throughout — nonnegativity is a structural premise of the theory.
