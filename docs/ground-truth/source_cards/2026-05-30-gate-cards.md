## arxiv:1909.03368

- **Claim:** Hewitt & Liang (2019), "Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks." A *control task* is defined relative to a linguistic task and **shares the linguistic task's output space Y**; it randomizes the **OUTPUT/labels** by sampling a control behavior `C(v)` independently at random for each word *type* v, so the token's output is a deterministic function of its type and "can only be learned if the probe memorizes the mapping" (Fig 1 caption; §2.3 "Properties of control tasks"). **Selectivity is defined as `linguistic task accuracy − control task accuracy`** (Fig 2 caption, verbatim). High selectivity = the probe's accuracy reflects structure in the *representation* rather than the *probe's own capacity to memorize word-type→label associations*. Empirically: popular MLP probes on ELMo are *not* selective (high control accuracy); linear/bilinear probes are more selective at similar accuracy; dropout does not reliably improve MLP selectivity but weight decay / low rank does; ELMo layer-2 probes are far more selective than layer-1. The paper explicitly frames control tasks as **complementary to, and distinct from, "random representation baselines" (Proj0 / Zhang & Bowman 2018)** — the latter randomize the *input representation*, control tasks randomize the *output* (§1; §3.5, lines on Proj0/ELMo2).
- **Neuro-AI relevance:** Directly relevant as the methodological basis the project is invoking for a *selectivity-style* control behind a role-recoverability headline (the G-D / gate0 gauge-control lineage: `notes/notes/2026-05-28-gate0-frame-a-valid-control-precommit.md`, `reports/gate0_2026-05-28/gate0_summary.md`). The deep idea — *a readout's success must be scored against a matched control that an "expressive enough" readout could pass by memorization alone, and the headline is the **difference**, not the raw accuracy* — is exactly the project's difference-in-differences / gauge-control instinct and its standing caution that "algebraic/decode success ≠ basin retrieval." It is also the correct citation for *why a raw `top_index_hits` recoverability number is uninterpretable without a paired control*.
- **Principle link:** principles.md "Headline Vs Drill-Down Discipline" (one headline = the **difference** metric, not a raw accuracy) and the "Proposal Citation Rule" / control-condition commitment ("Controls can be precommitted before retrieval or training" under the Anti-Homunculus Filter; "Do not trust a new mechanism until it survives a control condition," PROJECT_PLAN/CLAUDE.md). Selectivity = a precommitted difference-control, which is the passing shape.
- **Caution:** (1) The control here randomizes the **OUTPUT (labels)**, NOT the input. A *role-SHUFFLED cue* randomizes the **INPUT** — in Hewitt & Liang's own taxonomy that is the **random-representation baseline (Proj0)**, which they explicitly hold *separate* from control tasks. So citing this paper to license a role-shuffled-*cue* control is a category error: it supports a *label/role-assignment-shuffle* control, not an input-cue shuffle. (2) Selectivity is fundamentally about a **trained probe with tunable expressivity** (rank, width, dropout, weight decay); the whole construct measures *probe capacity to memorize*. An FHRR/MHN basin-membership / `top_index_hits` retrieval is a **fixed energy-descent readout with no trained, capacity-tunable probe**, so the quantity selectivity isolates (excess accuracy attributable to probe expressivity) has no native analog. (3) The paper is a diagnostic/interpretation tool, not a claim about basins, energy, or memory dynamics — it does not transfer as a *mechanism*, only as an *evaluation discipline*.
- **Transfer verdict:** transfers-with-caveats (as an *evaluation discipline*: "report a precommitted difference against a memorization-matched control, never a raw recoverability number"); **does-not-transfer** for the specific operationalization the task asks about — a **role-shuffled CUE** is an input randomization (Proj0-type random-representation baseline in this paper's taxonomy), not a Hewitt-Liang control task, and there is no capacity-tunable probe in a fixed FHRR/MHN `top_index_hits` readout for "selectivity" to control. A *role-label / role-binding shuffle that randomizes the role→slot assignment while preserving the output space* would be the faithful analog of a control task; a Selectivity-Delta of `top_index_hits` against THAT (not against a shuffled cue) would be a defensible borrowing, but it is analogy-level until built and is not yet shown by any `reports/NNN`.

## arxiv:2303.13957

- **Claim:** The opened paper is *"Factorizers for Distributed Sparse Block Codes"* (Hersche, Terzic, Karunaratne, Langenegger, Pouget, Cherubini, Benini, Sebastian, Rahimi; Neurosymbolic AI, 2023/rev. 2024) — NOT a paper titled "Generalized Sparse Block Codes" (that is the project's manifest alias). What it actually establishes: a **factorizer (BCF)** — a resonator-style iterative unbind/cleanup loop — for *Generalized Sparse Block Codes (GSBC)*. Verified from the body: (a) binding for GSBC is **blockwise circular convolution** (binary SBC: elementwise modulo-L sum of one-hot block offsets), preserving dimensionality and block sparsity; (b) the native similarity is **ℓ∞-based**, verbatim `s∞(x,y) := 1 − ℓ∞(x − y)` with `ℓ∞(a) = maxᵢ|a[i]|`, bounded in [0,1], =1 iff identical; (c) with a threshold nonlinearity + conditional random sampling, BCF reaches **operational capacity 5×10⁶** at D=512, B=4 blocks, F=2 factors (~15.7 avg iters), vs ~10³ for binary-SBC dot-product and a ~6× iteration speedup over dense-bipolar resonators at 10⁶ problem size (11.16 vs 68.47 iters). Crucially, (d) the **codebooks are FIXED and RANDOM** — block codewords are drawn by randomly activating one element per block; training only adapts the CNN→product-space *input mapping*, never the codebook geometry.
- **Neuro-AI relevance:** This is the primary behind the parked-GSBC un-park branch (`notes/notes/2026-05-30-gsbc-substrate-swap-kickoff.md`, "Parked: GSBC"). It supplies the two native primitives the kickoff seed makes binding-of-line-1: the ℓ∞ similarity `s∞ = 1 − ℓ∞(·)` and blockwise-circular binding/unbinding — the exact substitutes for the FHRR-cosine / per-element-normalized-mean-real-product Hopfield that produced the Report 066 GHRR confound (`reports/066_mqar_ghrr_bundle_first_discriminator.md`: a foreign algebra stored in the FHRR-shaped MHN settled near-uniform, `top_index_hits=0`, and faked success via algebraic decode). It is read-side: a factorizer/cleanup, the right primitive for *legibility* of already-written structure, and confirms that ℓ∞ activations are mostly small (the project's "structural zeros" intuition has an algebraic home here even though the paper does not name it).
- **Principle link:** Hard rule 4 (separate "supports" from "shown") and the anti-homunculus filter in `principles.md` / `CLAUDE.md`. The paper's factorizer is a clean local dynamic — iterative unbind → ℓ∞-similarity search → threshold/sample → rebundle — i.e. local geometry/settling, no supervisor arbitration (the ℓ∞ "zeros" are a metric property, not a thresholding decision by a module). It passes the anti-homunculus screen as a *read* mechanism. It does NOT touch a write objective, so it is silent on the project's actual open question.
- **Caution:** Three honesty flags. (1) **Title/identity mismatch** — the manifest/brainstorm call this "Generalized Sparse Block Codes"; the primary is the *factorizer* paper. Don't cite it as the GSBC algebra-definition paper; the algebra basis is Frady–Sommer 2020 (arxiv:2009.06734, not yet carded). (2) **"Block-local / confined crosstalk" is NOT established by this primary.** The opened body does not characterize interference as within-block vs global; that is a project-internal inference from block structure + the kickoff seed's own hedge ("GSBC relocates crosstalk into within-block competition; it does not eliminate it"). Treat block-confinement as a hypothesis to test, not a result this paper proved. (3) **Fixed random codebook** — the paper writes no learned structure, so it cannot speak to whether a consolidation objective can *write* a role basin (the Stage-1 go/no-go). The structural-zero property is a clean-one-hot regime; under bundling/renorm (the project's operating regime) GSBC products entangle — which is precisely *why* a factorizer is needed.
- **Transfer verdict:** does-not-transfer (as a write mechanism) / transfers-with-caveats (as a read-side factorizer only). The factorizer + ℓ∞ primitives transfer to an FHRR/MHN `top_index_hits` setting as a *legibility/cleanup* upgrade IFF role-discriminative structure has already been written into the substrate. It does NOT transfer to the active first-move question (does the consolidation objective write a recoverable role basin?), which the kickoff seed and Reports 062/063/064 show is the real blocker (`hit_role=0.000` is flat/absence, not graded/overlap). A cleaner reader cannot surface structure that consolidation never laid down. So: a READ-side legibility upgrade that writes no structure — never a substitute for a write objective. ## arxiv:2310.05644

- **Claim:** (read from primary HTML methods/results, not just abstract) The paper decomposes continual-learning accuracy loss in a 4-layer CNN — pretrained on CIFAR-10, then trained on 10 task-incremental CIFAR-100 splits with a fresh classification head per task — into three measured components. It trains *diagnostic readouts* (fresh linear classifiers) on the network's hidden representations after every training phase. (1) **Readout misalignment** = original-readout-performance (at task onset, t=0) minus freshly-trained diagnostic-readout performance; this is the *largest* component — the features still carry the information but the original output layer no longer reads it out correctly. (2) **True forgetting** = the drop that persists *even after* refitting the readout = irrecoverably lost task-relevant information ("only a small part of the information is irrecoverably lost"). (3) **Geometry-preserving changes** = representational shift captured by a Procrustes transformation; representational geometry is *partially conserved* under misalignment. All three scale with hidden-representation dimensionality. So the "production-vs-refit accuracy gap = readout misalignment" framing IS the paper's real, primary result — confirmed from the opened method, not inferred from the title.
- **Neuro-AI relevance:** Supplies a *diagnostic protocol template*, not a substrate result: freeze the representation, refit a fresh readout on a held-out split, and attribute the production-vs-refit gap to readout misalignment vs. the residual to true (irrecoverable) information loss. This is precisely the read-half logic the project's G-A frozen-substrate refit-readout gate wants to borrow to separate "the consolidation objective never wrote a recoverable basin" from "the basin is written but the top-1/production readout can't address it." The "refit recovers → readout defect, un-park GSBC as a read-side fix" branch of G-A is a faithful structural analogue of this paper's "readout misalignment" component.
- **Principle link:** Anti-Homunculus Filter (principles.md:33-44, 53-59): a diagnostic readout fit *offline on a held-out split that never selects at runtime* is a measurement of a local dynamic, not an arbiter — exempt by the same logic the project applies to top_index_hits/Wilson-CI readouts. Also Authority Order item 5 (principles.md:14) + Proposal Citation Rule (principles.md:75-84): this was a `link_only`/absent-from-manifest source whose primary was never opened; carding from primary before any numbered report cites it is the project's own hard rule against fabricated-citation drift.
- **Caution:** Three concrete mismatches gate the borrow. (a) **Substrate:** the paper studies a gradient-trained deep CNN with a softmax linear probe over Euclidean feature activations; the project's setting is an FHRR phasor codebook + Modern Hopfield basin with a top_index_hits/argmax-against-values readout. "Refit a linear classifier" and "refit a basin-membership readout" are not the same operation, and the paper establishes nothing about phasor/MHN geometry. (b) **"Refit fails → never written" is a reinterpretation, not the paper's claim.** In the paper, refit-fails ≡ *true forgetting* = information that *was* learned and then *erased/degraded by subsequent task learning*. The project's G-A gloss "structure truly never written" is about *failure-to-write on a single consolidation event* with no subsequent overwriting task — a different causal story the paper does not test. (c) **Continual/sequential confound:** the misalignment here is *induced by training on additional tasks*; the project's G-A toy freezes after one consolidation with no interfering second task, so the dominant mechanism the paper measures (drift from *new-task* learning) is largely absent. Treat the result as a methodology import; do not cite the paper's *quantitative* "largest component" finding as evidence about FHRR/MHN.
- **Transfer verdict:** transfers-with-caveats (as a diagnostic-protocol/decision-logic template) — but **analogy-only for the substrate-level claim**. The freeze-then-refit-fresh-readout protocol and the "production-minus-refit gap = readout misalignment vs. residual = irrecoverable loss" decomposition are real, paper-backed, and a legitimate methodological template for G-A's read-half. But the claim that this *result* holds for an FHRR/MHN basin-membership / top_index_hits setting is NOT established by the primary; the substrate, the readout family, and the "never written vs. erased" causal framing all differ. The project's own grill (02-forward-plan-FINAL.md:24, :157) already demoted this to citation-pending and re-grounded G-A's decision rule on internal Reports 066+111 + existing random-codebook/role-shuffle controls — that demotion is correct and should stand. G-A is a self-contained, internally-grounded gate; 2310.05644 motivates the protocol but must not carry the FHRR interpretation.

## arxiv:2312.04927

- **Claim:** Zoology (Arora, Eyuboglu, Timalsina, Johnson, Poli, Zou, Rudra, Ré 2023) formalizes **multi-query associative recall (MQAR)** as a write-then-read probe and uses it to explain the perplexity gap between attention and gated-convolution LMs. The opened primary (Definition 3.1) defines the task on a single input sequence x={x₀…x_{N-1}} of tokens: "for every query 1≤i<N, whether there exists a 0≤j<i such that uᵢ≡uⱼ. If so, output u_{j+1}." Worked example: `A 4 B 3 C 6 F 1 E 2 → A ? C ? F ? E ? B ?` — key→value pairs are written first, then *multiple* queries (not one terminal query) must each read back the value bound to a previously-seen key. The load-bearing empirical result (not the abstract's marketing) is a **capacity-vs-state-size law**: two-layer gated-convolution models need model dimension to scale at least linearly with sequence length (d≥N for >90% accuracy), while attention solves all tested lengths at near-constant d=64. Sweeps (§4.3) vary sequence length (64→512), number of KV pairs, vocab (up to 8192), and model dimension; accuracy is measured per-query on the value token. The controls are *architectural* (17 pretrained attention vs gated-conv models on the Pile) plus the synthetic MQAR sweep that isolates recall from other LM skills.
- **Neuro-AI relevance:** This is the single best external template for the project's missing eval doctrine. MQAR *is* a write-then-read associative-recall protocol with a built-in within-task control surface (vary N pairs, vary state size d, hold task fixed), which is exactly the shape the project already adopted ad hoc as its "external architecture-gate discriminator" in Reports 065/066/067 (`top_index_hits`, `scene_top_index_hits`, HRR-bundle positive control, perfect-cue memorization control, key-only discriminator). The primary supplies the principled justification for that harness: (1) the multi-query structure forces *recall*, not last-token memorization; (2) sweeping N at fixed d is the right axis to read off a capacity curve (mirrors Report 065's N∈{16..512} collapse-toward-chance and Report 067's scene-MHN identification bottleneck); (3) the d≥N law is the theory-side analogue of the project's empirical finding that key-only MHN retrieval over bind(k,v) decays to chance as N grows at fixed D.
- **Neuro-AI relevance (eval-doctrine answer):** Yes — MQAR informs a *fair* write-then-read eval. The fair-eval translation: write a batch of (role/key, filler/value) bindings into the substrate, then probe with each key alone and score whether the bound value's index is recovered. The "did the objective write recoverable role structure" question maps onto MQAR's per-query accuracy *with a same-test-set control*: run the identical query set against (a) the trained/objective substrate and (b) a precommitted control substrate (random codebook / shuffled bindings / no-write), holding keys, values, and query indices fixed per seed — precisely the same-substrate-per-cell control Reports 065/067 already implement. MQAR's own positive control (a solvable instance — the project's HRR-bundle 100% line) and memorization control (perfect-cue) are the right floor/ceiling brackets.
- **Principle link:** (1) "Contextual completion over token prediction" — MQAR scores recall of a written binding, not next-token prediction, matching the project's stated architectural target (CLAUDE.md "contextual-completion system, not a sequence-prediction system"). (2) Headline-vs-drill-down discipline + "Multi-metric panels without a named headline are not acceptable evidence": MQAR per-query recall@1 is a clean single headline candidate, and the project already factors it into headline (`top1`) + drill-downs (`scene_top_index_hits`, `content_top_index_hits`) in Report 067. (3) Anti-homunculus filter — passes by construction: MQAR is a *measurement of a fixed offline statistic* over a batch of probes; it reads no runtime metric to gate or branch any code path (Report 065 §Anti-homunculus check confirms the harness triggers no mechanism). It is therefore exempt as an offline/batch statistic. (4) Proposal-citation / authority-order rule: this card promotes the source from `link_only` (where it could only motivate a scan) toward load-bearing eval methodology.
- **Caution:** (1) **It is a sanity check, not a graduation headline, until a design spec promotes it.** Report 065 §Framing is explicit: adopting MQAR as the Phase 5 graduation criterion "would require an explicit spec amendment; this report does not propose one." The Phase 5 design-spec headline is ΔE (phase-5-unified-design.md:280-299), not MQAR recall. Do not let this card silently re-headline a phase. (2) **The primary is about sequence models (attention vs gated-conv transformers), not VSA/Hopfield substrates.** Its d≥N law is derived for gated-convolution state, not for FHRR phase capacity or MHN basin geometry — the numerical law does not port, only the *protocol shape and the capacity-curve methodology* do. (3) The primary's claim that "82% of the gap is explained by recall" is an LM-perplexity result on the Pile; it is irrelevant to basin-membership and must not be cited as evidence about the project's substrate. (4) Link-only-to-carded transition: this card is written from the opened HTML primary (Definition 3.1 quoted), so it is now load-bearing for *eval methodology*; it remains non-load-bearing for any substrate-capacity numerics. (5) MQAR was already run informally by the project before this card existed — the eval-doctrine value here is retroactive justification + standardization, not a new capability.
- **Transfer verdict:** **transfers-with-caveats.** The *protocol and control design* transfer cleanly to an FHRR/MHN basin-membership / `top_index_hits` setting — indeed the project independently reinvented it (Reports 065/066/067), and MQAR Definition 3.1's write-then-read multi-query structure is a near-exact match for "write bindings, probe with key alone, score value-index recovery with a same-test-set random/shuffled-codebook control." What does NOT transfer is the paper's *quantitative result* (the d≥N capacity law and the 82%-of-perplexity-gap claim), which is specific to gated-convolution sequence models and says nothing about FHRR bundle capacity or MHN key-only basin geometry. Use it to standardize the eval, the positive/memorization/chance controls, and the N-sweep capacity-curve reporting; do not use it as evidence about substrate behavior and do not promote MQAR recall to a phase headline without an explicit design-spec amendment.

## arxiv:2406.03980

It is a `link_only` position paper with no methodological prescription; treating its existence as "we have a negative-result methodology" would be a category error and would conflate it with 1909.03368. Load-bearing null-interpretation discipline must come from the project's own precommitted controls (random codebook, shuffled tokens, no-replay) plus a Hewitt-Liang-style upper-bound/selectivity arm — not from this paper.

## arxiv:2410.06232

- **Claim:** Dorrell & Whittington give an *analytical characterization of the optima* of a **linear** autoencoder with a **nonnegativity constraint on the latent** (z = W_in·s + b_in, s = W_out·z + b_out, z ≥ 0) and an energy penalty on activations + weight Frobenius norms (Eq. 1–2). Theorem 2.1: at the minima, the representation modularizes (each latent neuron reads only one source) **iff** a geometric "range" condition on the source support holds — informally, the support is "sufficiently spread" / range-independent (Corollary 2.3: each source attains its minimum independently of whether other sources are at their extrema). Theorem 2.2 restates this as: a covariance ellipse must lie entirely inside the convex hull of the data. The paper's load-bearing surprise is that this condition is **range independence, NOT statistical independence** of sources, and — verbatim — "Remarkably, they do not depend on the energy tradeoff parameter λ." So modularity is a property of the *optimal solution under nonnegativity + support geometry*, not something the penalty weight tunes. Nonnegativity is essential: Appendix G ("Biological Constraints are necessary for Modularity") shows removing z ≥ 0 (signed latents) breaks it — the mechanism is that under rectangular support a mixed (entangled) code needs a larger nonnegativity bias, i.e. costs more activation energy, than a modular code, so the nonneg energy floor is what selects modularity.
- **Neuro-AI relevance:** This is the theory cited by the project's range-shaped replay work (Reports 068/069/110/111). It is the formal backing for "data-shaping (rectangular joint support over role × content) can *force* role/content modularization" — a data-side, non-arbitration intervention consistent with the project's consolidation/replay reshaping-the-landscape commitment. It cleanly separates two distinct levers the project conflated: (a) a **storage-side / sampler variant** — make the *replayed* joint over (role, atom) rectangular (what Report 068 actually built and validated at the sampler level); and (b) the **training-objective variant** — minimize a nonnegative energy-efficient AE loss whose *optimum* modularizes given rectangular support (what the theorem is actually about, and what Report 068 explicitly deferred). The theorem speaks to (b); the project has only exercised (a).
- **Principle link:** principles.md "Continuous learning — replay consolidates, abstracts, and reshapes the landscape" + the Anti-Homunculus Filter passing shape "Sampling, replay, and consolidation probabilities are fixed functions of local state" and "Controls can be precommitted before training." Range-shaped data is a fixed offline/batch shaping of the training distribution, not a runtime metric-reader that gates behavior — it passes the anti-homunculus filter (data-shaping, not arbitration). Also touches "Energy efficiency — sparse activation": the modularity-selecting force IS the nonneg activation-energy floor.
- **Caution:** (1) The result is about the **optimum of a linear, nonnegatively-constrained autoencoder**, not a learning dynamic, not a Hopfield basin, and not a replay sampler — those are project-side analogies, not what is proven. (2) **Nonnegativity is load-bearing and the project's FHRR substrate violates it natively:** FHRR atoms are unit-magnitude complex phasors that compose additively in the angle domain (torch_fhrr.py:91) — inherently signed, not z ≥ 0. The theorem's energy-cost-of-mixing argument has no native analogue on a signed/unit-magnitude code; transferring it requires inserting a nonneg recoding layer (e.g. a ReLU read-out + weight decay) somewhere the modularity is supposed to live, which the project has not built. (3) λ-independence cuts both ways: it means you cannot tune modularity with a penalty weight — modularity is gated by support geometry, so if your replayed support is NOT rectangular you get nothing, and Report 068 showed 87% of rectangularized (role, atom) pairs have no backing trace (need rebind-on-the-fly). (4) link_only in the manifest; HTML opened this session but no local PDF carded, so do not treat equation/appendix numbers as load-bearing beyond what is quoted here.
- **Transfer verdict:** transfers-with-caveats — but only to the *training-objective* variant, and only if a nonnegative recoding layer is added; for the project's actual FHRR/MHN basin-membership / top_index_hits setting as currently built, it is **analogy-only**. The theorem does not say anything about Hopfield basin membership or top-index retrieval; it characterizes which linear-AE optima modularize. The project's empirical wins on basin retrieval (Report 067 bundle-first, 100% top-1) came from an architecture-side change, not from this theorem. Range-shaped replay (the data-side borrow) is validated only at the *sampler* level (Report 068, n=3, KL 2.08→0.013); whether it moves any downstream basin/ΔE metric is **unmeasured and deferred**, and the nonnegativity precondition for the theorem to bite is absent from the FHRR substrate.

## arxiv:2502.05164

- **Claim:** (from the opened HTML + PDF, not the abstract) The paper defines *in-context denoising*: a prompt is a context sequence `(X_1..X_L)` plus a noise-corrupted query `X̃ = X_{L+1} + Z`, `Z~N(0,σ²I)`, and the target is the clean `X_{L+1}`. They prove (Bayesian framework, restricted Gaussian-mixture case) that a single trained attention layer denoises *optimally*, and that at inference this layer is equivalent to **one gradient-descent step on a context-aware DAM/modern-Hopfield energy** `E(X_{1:L}, s) = (1/2α)‖s‖² − (1/β)·log Σ_t exp(β·X_tᵀs)`, with the GD step `s(t+1) = s(t) − γ∇_s E` **initialized at the noisy query `s(0) = x̃`**. The update moves the **query state** toward a low-energy point; the context tokens *are* the stored associative memories and are **not modified**. Training is **offline MSE** (`C(θ) = Σ_i ‖F(E^(i),θ) − x_{L+1}^(i)‖²`) over attention parameters `W_KQ, W_PV`, which parameterize the effective `α, β, γ` of the energy. The one-step update beats exact retrieval of any single context token or spurious local minimum — DAM "beyond pure retrieval."
- **Neuro-AI relevance:** Directly addresses candidate-2 (the predictive/denoising write). It is a clean existence proof that a *noisy→clean* mapping can be installed by **offline gradient descent that reshapes energy-landscape parameters** (`W_KQ, W_PV` ⇒ `α, β, γ`) rather than by a runtime if-then. The clean target is **cue-derived self-supervision** — `X_{L+1}` is the same draw from `p_X` that the noisy query corrupts — so there is **no external class label / no smuggled controller**. This is the FHRR-analog of "unbind→rebind a self-target": the denoiser learns to pull a corrupted observation back onto the clean manifold defined by the in-context memories.
- **Principle link:** Anti-Homunculus Filter (principles.md:33-59) — passes the "fixed dynamic replaces if-then" test: the same energy `E` always applies, the apparent decision lives in *settling/geometry* (one GD step on `E`), and the only "training" is an offline batch statistic over (noisy,clean) pairs — exempt from the thermostat rule. Also supports **Latent reasoning** (principles.md:26-27) — reasoning as an energy-state update before any decode — and **Continuous learning / landscape reshape** (principles.md:24-25): "replay consolidates, abstracts, and reshapes the landscape." The cue-derived target (not a label) is what keeps it controller-free.
- **Caution:** (1) The "memories" are **in-context prompt tokens retrieved fresh each forward pass**, not a persistent consolidated codebook — the offline write produces a *fixed denoiser policy* (the attention weights), it does NOT demonstrate a per-experience consolidation write into a durable Hopfield store. Do not read this as evidence that an online/replay write reshapes a standing landscape. (2) Optimality is proven only for **restricted Gaussian-mixture / single-layer** denoising; the headline is **MSE reconstruction of a continuous vector**, not discrete basin membership. (3) The GD step is on the **query state**, so "reshapes the landscape" is true only of the *offline* parameter-training; the *inference* step is a state update on a fixed landscape — keep these two timescales separate or the claim inflates. (4) `link_only`→ now opened; manifest still lists `authors: unknown` and the brainstorm-shorthand title — corrected here (true title "In-context denoising with one-layer transformers...", Smart/Bietti/Sengupta, ICML 2025).
- **Transfer verdict:** transfers-with-caveats. The *shape* of candidate-2 (offline-gradient noisy→clean write with a cue-derived self-target, no label, no runtime branch) is validated as principled and controller-free, and an FHRR-native unbind-then-rebind self-target is a faithful port of the cue-derived target. But the paper's *measured* result is continuous-MSE denoising over in-context prompt memories on a Gaussian mixture — it does NOT establish that this write improves **basin membership / top_index_hits** over a persistent emergent codebook. The project must run its own headline (basin retrieval / cap-coverage with controls); this source motivates and de-risks the mechanism's *form*, it does not stand in for the empirical demonstration.

## arxiv:2505.22749

- Claim: Spisak and Friston derive attractor networks from the free energy principle over a continuous-Bernoulli partition (units sigma_i in [-1,+1] subset R, all-to-all coupling J, NOT a binary spin net). The learning rule (Eq. 19) is dJ_ij proportional to sigma_i sigma_j minus L(b_i + sum_k J_ik sigma_k) sigma_j: a Hebbian observed-correlation term MINUS a predicted-correlation (anti-Hebbian) term, where L is the Langevin sigmoid. It is single-phase: the authors explicitly contrast it with contrastive divergence, noting it does not require separate clamped and free phases (sec 2.5) and instead uses the instantaneous pre/post correlation. Approximate orthogonalization of attractors emerges from the KL-to-prior complexity term in the variational free energy (sec 2.6), which penalizes overlapping attractors; the anti-Hebbian term subtracts already-predicted variance, analogous to Sanger rule for PCA. Random presentation gives symmetric/sparse couplings; sequential presentation gives asymmetric couplings and non-equilibrium steady state.
- Neuro-AI relevance: A single-phase, local, residual-error write rule that makes new attractors land approximately orthogonal to existing ones is relevant to emergent-codebook write dynamics and to an FHRR/MHN store wanting low attractor overlap without a separate decorrelation controller. The free-energy/complexity framing also models attractors-encode-prior-beliefs, relevant to Phase 3/5 role-prior vs content-prior work.
- Principle link: Anti-Homunculus Filter (principles.md:33-59): the update is a fixed local function of pre/post state minus the network own prediction, with no metric-reader gating a response and no supervisor choosing decorrelation; the decision to orthogonalize lives in the energy/complexity gradient. Also touches Latent reasoning and Continuous learning (principles.md:24-27).
- Caution: (1) The paper shows orthogonal attractors only, NOT role-vs-content disentanglement; do not borrow as evidence for codebook factorization. (2) Orthogonality is approximate and is an analytic/small-simulation result on the authors own continuous-Bernoulli substrate, not FHRR/MHN. (3) link_only with no local PDF: carded from the arXiv HTML render plus an independent review, not a checksummed primary; re-open before load-bearing use. (4) Orthogonal attractors is a coupling-matrix geometry claim, distinct from basin-membership / top_index_hits.
- Transfer verdict: transfers-with-caveats. The single-phase Hebbian-minus-predicted-correlation write is a concrete anti-homunculus-clean candidate-5 mechanism worth a precommitted scan, but its demonstrated result (approx-orthogonal attractors on a continuous-Bernoulli all-to-all net) is analogy-only with respect to FHRR/MHN basin-membership: the paper does not establish that this update improves top_index_hits or basin retrieval, and shows no role-vs-content disentanglement. Borrow the update form, not the orthogonality headline.

## pmc:PMC10484462

- **Claim:** The paper (Pang, Fusi et al., "Rapid memory encoding in a recurrent network model with BTSP") formalizes behavioral-timescale synaptic plasticity as a one-shot, weight-bounded rule: Δw = P(1−w)·f_P(θ) − D·w·f_D(θ), with w∈[0,1] (Eq. 2; recurrent form Eq. 3). Potentiation is multiplied by the headroom (1−w) and depression by the current weight w, so the rule has a per-synapse analytical fixed point μ_w = P/(P+D) that a single plateau pulse drives weights toward in one pass — no iterative gradient training. The plateau potential is a fixed-shape, time-locked instructive event (P(x(t))=1 during a plateau, 0 otherwise; ~300 ms; sourced from EC input / dendritic Ca²⁺ spike or induced by current injection), not a runtime threshold on firing rate or error. The eligibility kernels (f_P=1+cos θ, f_D=1−cos θ in the simplified analysis) make potentiation dominate for synapses co-active with the plateau and depression dominate elsewhere, so a single exposure produces a non-flat margin between potentiated and non-potentiated synapses. The recurrent CA3-like attractor stores bump states; capacity scales ~M² under sparse coding, and quenched interference between memories can stabilize rather than destroy attractors.
- **Neuro-AI relevance:** This is the cleanest available primary for a one-shot write rule whose "decision" lives entirely in fixed local dynamics — exactly the shape the project wants for fast emergent-codebook / role writes without a supervisor. The (1−w) potentiation / w depression asymmetry is a candidate mechanism for getting basin separation (a margin) in a single pass instead of accumulating it over replay epochs, relevant to how a new content/role trace could be inscribed into the FHRR+MHN landscape on first encounter.
- **Principle link:** Anti-Homunculus filter (principles.md:33-59): the plateau is an instructive *event* that gates a fixed update, not an `if metric then action` reader — the apparent "decide to write now" decision lives in the externally/stochastically timed plateau pulse and the fixed (1−w)/w geometry, with μ_w=P/(P+D) as the precommittable fixed point. Also supports "Continuous learning" (principles.md:24-25): live experience writes a trace in one shot, consolidation reshapes later.
- **Caution:** This is a rate-based abstract weight-map model (validated against a biophysical model), not a spiking or FHRR/MHN model — capacity and attractor-stability claims are derived for a CA3 ring-attractor with sparse binary-ish coding, not for unit-magnitude FHRR vectors or a Modern Hopfield softmax energy. The asymmetric rule produces a *synaptic* margin; the project's standing caution applies — synaptic/weight separation is not the same as Hopfield basin retrieval or top_index_hits, and that gap is unproven here. The plateau is "instructive" only because it is exogenous/stochastic; if a future port triggers the plateau by reading a project metric at runtime, that would reintroduce a thermostat and the anti-homunculus pass would fail.
- **Transfer verdict:** transfers-with-caveats — the *rule form* (bounded one-shot Δw with (1−w)/w asymmetry, fixed point P/(P+D), plateau as fixed-shape instructive gate) transfers as a mechanism template and an anti-homunculus-clean exemplar; the *demonstrated result* (M² capacity, bump-attractor stability) does not transfer to an FHRR/MHN basin-membership setting without re-derivation on the actual substrate, and "creates a non-flat margin in one pass" is shown for synaptic weights, not for MHN basin membership / top_index_hits. Any project use must re-run the margin claim against cap-coverage / top_index_hits with a control before it is load-bearing.
