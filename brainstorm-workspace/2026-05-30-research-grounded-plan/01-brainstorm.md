### 1. Frozen-substrate refit-readout gate (run FIRST)

### 2. Swap-reconstruction consolidation (ArSyD / Soft-TPR / Dual-Swap shape)
- **novelty:** new-source · **phases:** Stage-1 toy (pre-Phase-5-prime), Phase 4 consolidation objective
- **what:** Replace the role-shuffled-negative contrastive framing with encode-swap-decode. Build pairs of bound items that share a role but differ in one filler; offline and batch, exchange the differing filler contribution and require native FHRR unbind-then-rebind to reconstruct both swapped items. Modularity falls out by construction (ArSyD uses only reconstruction MSE; Soft-TPR adds a filler-swap term). The swap negative is a real other-example filler, never the model own predicted_id.
- **why:** This is the precise FHRR-native version of the role-shuffled negative the diagnosis rank-1 wanted but could not specify, and it structurally avoids the documented phase3b failure, which mined its negative from the learner own predicted_id (a self-mined collapsing negative, Fail Rate 1.0 at single seed 17). Three independent communities converge that fixed-random roles are fine and the bug is the objective lacking a swap term.
- **anti-homunculus:** Offline-statistic. Reconstruction pressure on swapped pairs forces each role-slot to carry exactly one filler; a leaked slot makes the swapped reconstruction wrong. The decision lives in the swapped-reconstruction energy minimum; negatives are precommitted real-example swaps (augmentation, not runtime selection). Control: a no-swap ablation must not modularize; random-codebook must fail.
- **cheapest test:** Stage-1 toy, 4 roles, small fixed-random codebook. Headline native top_index_hits (basin-membership, not top-1, per Report 066) above chance. Controls: no-swap ablation Delta near zero; random-codebook fail; shuffled role-atom pairing destroys recoverability; lower-D arm for the capacity wall.
- **sources:** arxiv:2412.19847, arxiv:2412.04671, arxiv:1805.10583, reports/066, reports/phase3b_error_driven/03_phase3b_error_driven.md

### 4. PREDICTIVE consolidation write (DreamWeaver / JEPA port, cue-derived self-target)
- **novelty:** new-source · **phases:** Stage-1 toy (pre-Phase-5-prime), Phase 4 consolidation objective
- **what:** Replace the centroid-mean write with a self-supervised predictive target: from a partial role-cue r, predict the other co-occurring filler in the same window (cue-derived target via FHRR unbind-then-rebind, with an EMA or stop-grad on the target). DreamWeaver finds that prediction, not present-reconstruction, is the active ingredient that writes role structure, because the role is the only thing selecting which future follows.
- **why:** A store-as-is target is minimized by the members-mean (the flat EBM-2006 zero-margin surface the project has); a predictive role-to-co-filler target is minimized only if the representation keeps whatever distinguishes the futures, the role axis. This sharpens diagnosis rank-2 (the PAM missing between-pattern association) and sidesteps negative-construction entirely.
- **anti-homunculus:** Local-dynamic. Clean only because the target is a fixed function of the cue or data (a cue-derived self-target), not a label; a label would be a smuggled controller. The decision lives in the predictive-coding settling dynamic. Control: shuffled role-filler pairing must destroy recoverability; test held-out roles per the AAR no-inductive-transfer caution.
- **cheapest test:** Stage-1 toy: consolidate under centroid-mean (must stay flat) versus predictive cue-to-co-filler with an EMA target. Readout top_index_hits above chance; lower-D arm. Port risk to flag: every PC or JEPA primary writes into an external Euclidean predictor; the FHRR-native unbind-then-rebind self-target is the unproven step.
- **sources:** arxiv:2501.14174, pdf:pam-2026, arxiv:2502.05164

### 5. FEP single-phase self-orthogonalizing consolidation (anti-Hebbian is the missing margin)
- **novelty:** in-corpus · **phases:** Stage-1 toy (pre-Phase-5-prime), Phase 4 consolidation objective
- **what:** Replace the members-mean centroid with the FEP self-orthogonalizing coupling update (arxiv:2505.22749 Eq.25): observed correlation (Hebbian) minus predicted correlation (anti-Hebbian, via the Langevin function). The anti-Hebbian term is the contrastive pull-up EBM-2006 prescribes, derived from free-energy minimization, and it runs in a single phase (no clamped/free split), which defuses the rank-1 risk that EqProp needs symmetric real fixed-points unavailable to complex phasors.
- **why:** It supplies the anti-overlap pressure the centroid-mean lacks, with a scoping correction the briefs surface: Reports 111 and 068 falsified the Dorrell range replay-sampler, a different mechanism; a grep of 111 and 068 for FEP, Friston, Spisak, or 2505.22749 returns empty (re-verified this session), so the FEP write has an empty falsification record here. Orthogonalization emerges from the KL-to-prior complexity term; the paper proves Hebbian-alone cannot do it.
- **anti-homunculus:** Local-dynamic. The write is the coupling matrix J updated by Hebbian-minus-predicted-correlation; new bindings are forced to encode residual variance orthogonal to learned directions (Sanger-PCA-like). Control (doubles as the anti-homunculus proof): ablate the anti-Hebbian or complexity term and it must fail to orthogonalize. Random-codebook fail; shuffled-role gauge dE near zero; lower-D arm.
- **cheapest test:** Port the github fep-attractor-network minimal demo (25 units, 2 patterns) to the FHRR toy; readout top_index_hits above chance. Caution: arxiv:2505.22749 is link_only; card it from the opened HTML primary before it is load-bearing.
- **sources:** arxiv:2505.22749, pdf:energy-based-learning-2006, reports/111, reports/068

## Cross-cutting themes
- WRITE-THEN-READ is the missing experimental primitive. Every load-bearing idea writes with a candidate objective then reads with basin-membership (top_index_hits, not top-1 cosine, per Report 066) on a held-out split. The project has only ever measured storage-side deltas (Frame-B and Phase-5-prime deltaE) or top-1; Report 111 range_presettle (d_eff plus 27 but held-out CIs cross zero, novelty without retrieval) is in-house proof that storage-changed and recoverable-changed are dissociable. The read side is the only side that counts and it has never been run as a positive test.
- The active ingredient is the write data or target, not a magnitude knob. Three independent communities (bio-autoencoders or Dorrell, vision-disentanglement, object-centric slots) converge that the support or range of role-filler recombinations, surfaced via swap, prediction, or rectangular support, drives factorization, not independence and not activity-penalty magnitude (the Dorrell penalty-weight-independence theorem is a built-in falsifier). This corroborates the flat-or-absence diagnosis: a centroid-mean never exposes the model to the recombination spread, so no margin tuning can rescue it.
- Anti-homunculus-clean writes come in two exempt shapes: offline-statistic (swap-reconstruction, separation loss, rectangular-support AE, compositional-consistency, all batch passes STATUS permits) and local-dynamic (FEP self-orthogonalization, BTSP plateau, predictive settling, EM clustering). ## Challenges & counterarguments
- THE PIVOT CASE (structurally too weak): the effect may be below the substrate signal floor. Frame-B consolidation injects irreducible variance (sigma_A 0.157, Report 118 closed the landscape lever), Phase-5 hit_role is 0.000 across four families with sharpening making it worse, and the rank-1 contrastive write was already built and failed (phase3b Fail Rate 1.0). If Stage-1 is null across the contrastive and predictive families and a lower-D control, the disciplined move is to pivot (or accept a much weaker contextual-completion claim), not keep adding objective terms. The diagnosis itself names this escalation branch.
- The never-written verdict is one rung stronger than the record proves, and the single cheapest test (the frozen-substrate refit readout) could falsify the entire objective-rewrite program. If a fresh readout recovers role-target above chance on the frozen centroid-mean substrate, the locus is the readout (top-1 cosine), not the consolidation objective. That would un-park GSBC and make every write-side idea premature. This test argues against committing to rank-1 before running it.
- The binding-algebra wall (065 and 066: no key-only basins for any operator; GHRR-native tix 0 of 3072) is not excluded and is upstream of any consolidation objective. If the FHRR binding algebra cannot support key-only basins at all, then no consolidation objective fixes it; the right scope becomes the deeper substrate question (rebuild-from-1 or substrate swap), not a surgical objective change. The objective-rewrite ideas implicitly assume the algebra can hold a basin; the BTSP key-only test is the only idea that probes this directly.
- The just-add-a-contrastive-term move is verified as already tried and failed. The error_driven_learner push/pull (lr_pull 0.1, lr_push 0.05, about 29 events, single seed 17, Fail Rate 1.0) means rank-1 is not untried. The live unresolved risk is precisely the part that failed: constructing the most-offending negative in complex-phasor FHRR space and whether complex phasors hold the margin. The swap-negative and single-phase (FEP or dual-prop) variants are the only honest ways to claim a different experiment, not a re-run of a known null.
- Possible STALE STATUS, surfaced not papered over: STATUS says the range-shaped-replay lane is closed, and the diagnosis rank-3 calls Dorrell partially falsified by Report 111. But Report 111 closed a storage-side post-settle proxy; the actual Dorrell nonneg-autoencoder training objective (068 line 188 deferred it as a Phase 5-prime design question) and the FEP self-orthogonalizing write (a grep of 111 and 068 returns empty for it) have never been run. Treating the range or orthogonalization lever as closed-by-association mirrors the documented freq-weighted-alpha walk-back. The user should confirm the training-objective variant status before it is dismissed.
- Load-bearing risk: the spine rests on stale_pdf EBM-2006, but its collapse theorem applies to architectures lacking an automatic pull-up (Fig 9b) and is collapse-safe for simple regression (Eq 29); whether the project centroid-mean is the collapse-prone regime is an analogy, not a theorem about FHRR or MHN consolidation. The most exciting writer mechanisms (ArSyD, FEP, Dorrell, BTSP, separation-loss) are all link_only or uncarded. Carding two or three (priority: ArSyD 2412.19847 and Modular-Compositional-Bias 2510.21402 as FHRR-family swap anchors; FEP 2505.22749 for the single-phase property) is a prerequisite, not optional, before any is built as load-bearing.

## Rabbit holes
- Swapping the whole substrate to GSBC as a fix. GSBC (2303.13957, opened) is a read-side legibility upgrade only: fixed-random codebooks plus a pure iterative resonator readout, with only a CNN front-end trained. It writes nothing. Treating a readout swap as a write fix is a category error; GSBC stays parked to Stage-2 and un-parks only if Stage-1 shows a written-but-illegible basin (the overlap world). The lower-D control arm adjudicates this for free.
- Chasing EqProp or two-phase contrastive-Hebbian on complex phasors. The diagnosis already flags that EqProp assumes symmetric real fixed-points not obviously true for FHRR. Do not sink time into a two-phase clamped or free port; the single-phase variants (FEP self-orthogonalization, dual-propagation Hoier 2024, temporal-contrastive Falk 2025) sidestep the two-phase-symmetry problem and are the right entry point if a margin write is pursued.
- Tuning the activity or energy penalty weight in any range or Dorrell experiment. The opened Dorrell HTML proves modularity is independent of the penalty weight; the load-bearing ingredients are nonnegativity and rectangular support. Sweeping the weight for a modularity effect is chasing a red herring; at most it is a control (modularity must be invariant to the weight, else you found an activity-penalty artifact).
- Re-litigating the Frame-B slope-DiD, level-DiD, or window-averaging arithmetic. Those three levers are closed this session (Reports 116, 117, 118, multi-agent verified, variance recomputed to exact match). The Frame-B variance finding is a symptom (consolidation injects variance) pointing at the same root cause; re-deriving its economics is not where the open question lives. The open question is the Stage-1 write-then-read toy.
- Building the full Phase-4 consolidation integration before the Stage-1 toy. Report 068 deferred the integration for good reason: it is expensive. The cheap toy (4 roles, small codebook, basin-membership readout, lower-D control) is the go/no-go; do not scale to wikitext or full integration until Stage-1 writes a recoverable basin. Scaling a null is wasted compute.
- Carding all the link_only sources before running anything. Only two or three cards are prerequisites for the first experiments (ArSyD or Modular-Compositional-Bias for the swap write; FEP for single-phase). The frozen-refit-readout gate and the phase3b swap-negative A/B need no new card; they reuse existing code and stale_pdf anchors. Front-load the cheap no-card experiments; card on demand as each mechanism reaches load-bearing.
