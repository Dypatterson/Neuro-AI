# Research angle: How to EVALUATE "did the consolidation objective write recoverable role structure?" — the cheapest decisive write-then-read test + control, and continual-learning eval pitfalls. The corpus already names the trap (Report 066: top-1 cosine vs basin-membership disagree ~95 pts) but has NO load-bearing eval methodology: every eval source in literature_matrix.md row "Evaluation and external sanity checks" (MQAR/Zoology 2312.04927, Negative Results 2406.03980, ParetoCL 2503.23390, VAE+MHN-CLS 2507.11393) is link_only and not carded. I bring the missing eval discipline: a selectivity-style Delta readout (real-minus-control), a frozen-representation diagnostic-readout that separates "never written" from "written-but-unreadable", a causal-intervention bar above mere decodability, and the specific continual-learning pitfalls (task-recency bias, dimensional collapse, label-free disentanglement impossibility) that would make a Stage-1 write-then-read toy lie.

## Key findings
- (NEW) Hewitt & Liang (2019) control tasks give the canonical formalization of the project's required control: a control task maps the SAME inputs to RANDOM outputs, and SELECTIVITY = Delta = (real-task accuracy minus control-task accuracy). A readout that reads structure must have high real-task and LOW control-task accuracy; a high-capacity readout that memorizes (e.g. word identity) scores high on both, so low Delta means not selective. This operationalizes the project's random-codebook/shuffled-role control as a NUMBER (Delta), not a binary, and catches the Report 066 failure mode of a 100% top-1 that also fires on a random control.  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03368>
- (NEW) Diagnosing Catastrophe (2310.05644, abstract page opened): forgetting accuracy loss decomposes into (1) readout MISALIGNMENT [dominant], (2) preserved-but-shifted geometry, (3) small irreversible loss. Method: FREEZE hidden representations after each phase and RETRAIN a fresh linear readout; gap between original-readout and refit-readout accuracy = misalignment, not true forgetting. This is the single most on-target eval primitive for the project's open question: it separates consolidation-never-WROTE-role-structure (refit ALSO fails, matches FLAT diagnosis) from structure-present-but-top-1-cosine-cannot-READ-it (refit RECOVERS, diagnosis over-strong). The diagnosis itself flags it is reached by elimination not a positive write-then-read; a frozen-rep refit readout IS that positive test.  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05644>
- (NEW) Causal-probing reliability literature (amnesic probing 2006.00995; causal-probing-reliability 2408.15510, opened): decodability is NECESSARY-not-sufficient; conventional probing performance is not correlated to task importance, a feature can be linearly decodable yet causally unused. The stronger bar is INTERVENTION: erase/ablate the putative role direction and check downstream retrieval degrades. For the project a role basin counts as written-and-load-bearing only if removing it (or unbinding with the WRONG role) breaks recovery, a within-substrate analog stronger than top_index_hits and immune to the lucky-algebraic-decode confound Report 066 found (7% of FHRR correct top-1 were wrong-basin-but-lucky-unbind).  <https://arxiv.org/html/2408.15510v3>
- (NEW) Mechanistic evaluation of Transformers/SSMs on associative recall (2505.15105) introduces ATR (Associative Treecall): a PCFG-generated HIERARCHICAL recall task where keys/values are NOT adjacent, forcing TRUE non-positional retrieval (vs MQAR where position can leak the answer). It argues task accuracy alone is misleading and uses causal interventions to track emergence of the correct association mechanism beyond observed accuracy. Relevant because role binding is precisely non-positional content-addressed recall; a positional shortcut would let a toy pass without writing role structure. Shuffle-the-tree is a ready negative control.  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15105>
- (IN-CORPUS) Kernel/high-capacity Hopfield attractor analysis (2511.13053 Pinnacle Sharpness, IN corpus as link-only stub tags hopfield/sharpness; companion 2505.01218): margin-maximizing kernel objectives DEEPEN basins and SUPPRESS spurious attractors, whereas Hebbian-style storage creates noisy local minima, and crucially static metrics like margin or final capacity rarely investigate the DYNAMICAL mechanism of transient recall. Two payoffs: (a) corroborates rank-1 contrastive/margin objective over centroid-mean at attractor-geometry level; (b) supplies a native energy-landscape readout (basin sharpness/depth-to-neighbor) that is a measurement-of-a-local-dynamic, anti-homunculus-clean, decoupling deltaE-moved from right-basin-retrieved, the exact gap the diagnosis flags.  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13053>
- (NEW) Collapse taxonomy (mdpi math13182986 2025; Dimensional Collapse 2110.09348): COMPLETE collapse (all reps to one point / zero-margin flat surface) is detectable by feature variance/cosine, but DIMENSIONAL collapse (reps in a low-D subspace) is NOT reliably caught by variance/cosine, and participation ratio lacks standardized thresholds and varies across implementations. For the project the FLAT/zero-margin diagnosis is textbook COMPLETE collapse (the EBM-2006 push-down-only surface), the GOOD case for detection: hit_role 0.000 plus monotone-negative deltaE under sharpening is the unambiguous complete-collapse signature. But it cautions the project's existing participation-ratio drill-down cannot alone adjudicate a future partial (dimensional) write; pair PR with selectivity-Delta and refit-readout.  <https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/13/18/2986>
- (NEW) DCI disentanglement metric (Eastwood & Williams; survey 2410.03056) and Locatello Sober Look impossibility (2010.14766): DCI-Disentanglement = entropy of normalized importance weights of each latent dim for predicting each ground-truth factor (modularity), Completeness symmetric, Explicitness = ROC-AUC of one-vs-rest logistic readout. CRITICAL caution: disentangled representations cannot be IDENTIFIED without ground-truth labels; unsupervised modularity is unfalsifiable without a factor handle. This ENABLES the project (the Stage-1 toy HAS ground-truth roles so DCI is computable and label-free impossibility does NOT bite) and WARNS against any modularity-from-data claim (Dorrell-Whittington range lever) lacking a known-factor probe, mirroring the project's own gauge-vacuous-control finding.  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.14766>
- (NEW) Continual-learning readout pitfalls (task-recency bias 2302.08243, 2310.08855; stability-gap/classification-head WACV2025 2411.04723): replay/finetune-with-linear-head predictions are biased toward the MOST RECENT task's classes; Nearest-Mean-Classifier (NMC) readout has much lower recency bias than a linear head. For the Frame-B continual Growing Codebook eval and any Stage-1 sequential write, a top-1-cosine-over-codebook readout is structurally a linear head and will exhibit recency bias toward last-consolidated roles; an NMC/prototype-distance readout (which basin-membership already is) is the recency-robust choice. A concrete eval-design reason basin-membership beats top-1 beyond the Report 066 confound.  <https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04723>
- (IN-CORPUS) Project-internal anchor re-read (Report 066): top1 vs top_index_hits (tix) diverge by ~95 points; the GHRR cell showed top1=1.000 but tix~6% with weight-entropy~1.0 (uniform mixture, NOT basin retrieval); FHRR key-only top1=11.1% but tix=4.2% (7% were wrong-basin-but-lucky-algebraic-unbind); bundle-first is the ONLY positive rescue (tix=3072/3072, entropy=0). Empirically establishes for THIS project that the decisive readout is basin-membership + weight-entropy, and that a sharp basin on the WRONG pattern (entropy=0, tix=0 in GHRR-native) passes top-1 sanity but fails the real test.  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03368>
- (IN-CORPUS) Project-internal anchor re-read (Report 111): the range_presettle POSITIVE CONTROL wrote large novelty into storage (d_eff +19.4 to +27.9, stored_near 0.014-0.024) yet held-out retrieval did NOT follow (top1/topk CIs cross 0), labeled novelty without retrieval/utility. Project-internal proof that STORAGE-CHANGED and RECOVERABILITY-CHANGED are dissociable; any write-then-read eval MUST measure the READ (held-out recoverability), not the storage-geometry delta; d_eff/novelty alone would have falsely greenlit the lane. Note both the Frame-B headline (deltaE/variance) and the Phase-5-prime headline (deltaE) are storage-side energy deltas, not held-out recoverability.  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05644>

## New sources
- **Designing and Interpreting Probes with Control Tasks (Hewitt & Liang)** (2019) <https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03368> — Canonical SELECTIVITY metric Delta=(real minus random-control) accuracy. Turns the project's required random-codebook/shuffled-role control from a binary into a quantitative gate and catches high-capacity readouts that memorize rather than read written structure. Should be carded; methodology backbone for the Stage-1 readout.
- **Diagnosing Catastrophe: accuracy loss accounted for by readout misalignment** (2023) <https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05644> — Freeze-representations + refit-fresh-readout primitive that SEPARATES true absence (never written) from readout misalignment (written-but-unreadable). The direct positive test for the diagnosis's elimination-only weak point. Card it; phase3/phase4/phase5-prime relevant.
- **Mechanistic evaluation of Transformers and state space models (incl. ATR / Associative Treecall)** (2025) <https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15105> — ATR = PCFG hierarchical non-positional recall task, a harder position-shortcut-proof associative-recall benchmark than MQAR (already an uncarded link_only in the corpus). Gives a ready negative control (shuffle the tree) and a causal-intervention eval ethos matching the project's anti-top-1 lesson.
- **Measuring the Reliability of Causal Probing Methods (and Amnesic Probing, Elazar 2006.00995)** (2024) <https://arxiv.org/html/2408.15510v3> — Establishes decodability != causal use, and that intervention/ablation is the stronger bar. Motivates a within-substrate ablation readout (erase role direction / unbind with wrong role) above top_index_hits, immune to the lucky-algebraic-decode confound Report 066 found.
- **A Taxonomy and Theoretical Analysis of Collapse Phenomena in Unsupervised Representation Learning** (2025) <https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/13/18/2986> — Distinguishes complete vs dimensional collapse and warns participation-ratio thresholds are non-standardized. Confirms the FLAT diagnosis is detectable complete-collapse, but cautions the existing PR drill-down cannot adjudicate a partial write; pair PR with selectivity-Delta and refit-readout.
- **A Sober Look at the Unsupervised Learning of Disentangled Representations (Locatello et al.) + DCI survey 2410.03056** (2019) <https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.14766> — DCI (modularity = importance-weight entropy over known factors) is a label-using role-discriminability metric usable in the Stage-1 toy (roles are ground-truth). The impossibility result is the caution: any modularity-from-data lever (range/Dorrell-Whittington) needs a known-factor probe or it is unfalsifiable, mirroring the project's gauge-vacuous-control finding.

## Concrete ideas

- **FROZEN-SUBSTRATE REFIT-READOUT GATE (the decisive elimination-breaker). Before/alongside any objective rewrite, run the consolidation as-is, FREEZE the resulting substrate, then refit a fresh basin-membership readout (or a small linear probe) on held-out role cues and measure role-target recoverability. Per Diagnosing-Catastrophe (2310.05644) the gap between the production top-1 readout and the refit readout = readout misalignment; if the refit ALSO fails then structure truly never written (FLAT confirmed); if it RECOVERS then the defect is the readout not the objective. This is a READOUT/eval, not a new mechanism: it adds nothing to the architecture, it MEASURES the existing consolidation's local geometry.** [offline-statistic; Stage-1 toy (pre-Phase-5-prime; legitimately runnable, fence stays down). Also retro-applicable to Frame-B Phase 3.]
  - writes structure: It does NOT write structure; it is the read half of write-then-read. It adjudicates whether the EXISTING phase4 centroid-mean consolidation already wrote latent role-discriminative structure that top-1 cosine cannot surface.
  - test: Reuse the phase4 consolidation on the 4-role toy; freeze; refit a prototype/NMC readout on a held-out split; report Delta-recoverability(refit minus production-top1) with Wilson CI, plus a random-codebook control (Delta must be ~0 there). One afternoon, no new training mechanism. Run this BEFORE the contrastive rewrite.

- **SELECTIVITY-Delta AS THE STAGE-1 HEADLINE (one number, not a panel). Define the Stage-1 headline as Delta = recoverability(true-role cue) minus recoverability(role-SHUFFLED cue), measured by basin-membership (top_index_hits), with CI strictly > 0. Per Hewitt-Liang (1909.03368) this IS the selectivity control; per the diagnosis it doubles as the role-shuffled-negative the failed phase3b lacked. A contrastive/margin offline consolidation passes only if Delta>0 AND the no-negatives ablation gives Delta~0 AND random-codebook gives Delta~0.** [offline-statistic; Stage-1 toy headline metric. Replaces top-1 hit_role with a selectivity-difference.]
  - writes structure: The headline forces the WRITE side (the offline contrastive-Hebbian/EqProp pass with role-shuffled negatives, ranked #1 in the diagnosis) to produce a basin whose recoverability is ROLE-SPECIFIC: a shuffled role must NOT recover. Writing role-discriminative structure is definitionally what makes Delta>0.
  - test: On the 4-role toy: run contrastive consolidation; compute Delta over basin-membership for true vs shuffled-role cues; ablations (no-negatives, random-codebook) must collapse Delta to 0; add a lower-D arm (the diagnosis's capacity-wall control). Basin-membership not top-1 per Report 066.

- **WITHIN-SUBSTRATE ABLATION READOUT (decodability is not enough). Above basin-membership add a causal check per the causal-probing literature (2408.15510, amnesic 2006.00995): after consolidation, unbind the cue with the WRONG role (or project out the recovered role direction) and confirm recoverability DROPS to chance. A role basin counts as written-and-load-bearing only if the right role is necessary not merely sufficient: this kills the Report 066 lucky-algebraic-unbind confound (7% of FHRR top-1 were wrong-basin-but-lucky).** [offline-statistic; Stage-1 toy (readout panel, drill-down under the selectivity-Delta headline).]
  - writes structure: It is a readout that RAISES THE BAR on what the write must achieve: the consolidation must produce a basin causally keyed to the correct role, not one any unbind can stumble into. It disciplines the write objective without arbitrating at runtime.
  - test: Same toy; add a wrong-role-unbind arm and a role-direction-ablation arm; both must drop recoverability to chance while true-role stays high. Pure post-hoc analysis over stored states, no runtime branching.

- **NATIVE BASIN-SHARPNESS ENERGY READOUT instead of deltaE-as-headline. Adopt the kernel-Hopfield pinnacle/basin-sharpness geometry (2511.13053, 2505.01218) as the energy-side drill-down: measure depth-to-nearest-neighbor and curvature at the recovered attractor, NOT a global energy delta. The diagnosis notes deltaE measures something real that is not role-target retrieval and never graduated; basin-sharpness around the ROLE-TARGET attractor is the energy quantity that actually tracks retrieval.** [local-dynamic; Stage-1 toy energy drill-down; replaces the never-graduated deltaE framing with per-basin sharpness measured at the role target.]
  - writes structure: Diagnostic only, but it tells you WHETHER the margin/contrastive write deepened the role basin (the EBM-2006 pull-up effect) vs left the flat zero-margin surface. The energy-geometry confirmation that a contrastive write did what EBM-2006 predicts.
  - test: After consolidation compute settling-curvature/depth at the role-target basin vs a random basin on the toy; contrastive arm should deepen the role basin, centroid-mean arm should leave it flat (~zero margin). Measurement of the existing settling dynamic, no new module.

- **ATR-STYLE NON-POSITIONAL NEGATIVE CONTROL to prevent a positional shortcut from faking a pass. Per the mechanistic-eval paper (2505.15105) MQAR can be solved by position; role binding must be content-addressed. Add a control where role-cue position is randomized / the co-occurrence tree is shuffled: recoverability must DROP. If a toy writes role structure but survives tree-shuffle, it learned position not roles.** [offline-statistic; Stage-1 toy control; also informs Frame-B Phase 3 (whose stream-shuffle control was found gauge-vacuous; a content-vs-position shuffle is a non-vacuous alternative).]
  - writes structure: It does not write; it is a falsifier ensuring the write produced genuinely non-positional content-addressed role basins (the architectural target: contextual completion not sequence prediction per PROJECT_PLAN).
  - test: Add a shuffle-the-co-occurrence-structure arm to the Stage-1 toy; recoverability must collapse. Cheap: one extra data-generation switch. Aligns with Report 068 range-shaped sampler infrastructure.

- **NMC/PROTOTYPE READOUT (recency-robust) for any SEQUENTIAL consolidation eval. Per task-recency-bias CL literature (2411.04723, 2310.08855) a linear-head/top-1-cosine readout in sequential writes is biased toward the last-consolidated role. Use nearest-mean-classifier/prototype-distance (which basin-membership already approximates) as the standing readout for Frame-B continual eval and any multi-event Stage-1.** [offline-statistic; Frame-B Phase 3 continual eval; multi-event Stage-1 toy.]
  - writes structure: Readout only, but it prevents a FALSE no-role-structure verdict that is actually recency bias, and a FALSE structure-written that is just last-role dominance. Protects the integrity of the write-then-read verdict under continual consolidation.
  - test: On a 4-event sequential toy compare top-1-over-codebook vs NMC readout recency bias (per-role recoverability vs write-order); top-1 should show last-role bias, NMC should be flat. Confirms readout choice not a mechanism.

## Surprises
- The corpus's own eval doctrine is hollow: literature_matrix.md confidently routes MQAR, continual-learning baselines, negative result discipline to five source_ids, but every eval-row link_only source is an uncarded stub (checksum null, local_pdf_path null). The project has been gesturing at an eval methodology it has not actually read. The selectivity/refit-readout discipline that would make Stage-1 decisive is NOT yet in the corpus at all.
- The strongest available test directly THREATENS the diagnosis headline framing. The diagnosis calibrates that never-written is one rung stronger than proven and rests on elimination. The Diagnosing-Catastrophe refit-readout is the cheap experiment that could FALSIFY it: if a fresh readout refit on the FROZEN post-consolidation substrate recovers role-target above chance, then structure WAS written and the locus is the READOUT (top-1 cosine), not the consolidation objective, flipping rank-1 (contrastive objective) away from being the primary fix. Highest-value single test and it argues AGAINST committing to the objective-rewrite before running it.
- Report 111's range_presettle positive control is an in-house instance of the exact write-then-read decoupling the literature warns about: storage novelty went STRONGLY positive (d_eff +27) while held-out recoverability stayed at zero. The project already has empirical proof that a storage-geometry delta can be large and meaningless, yet the Frame-B headline (deltaE/variance) and the Phase-5-prime headline (deltaE) are both storage-side energy deltas, not held-out recoverability. The eval literature says the READ side is the only side that counts.
- Task-recency bias gives an independent, non-Report-066 reason top-1-over-codebook is the wrong readout: a linear-head readout in any SEQUENTIAL consolidation (Frame-B continual Growing Codebook) is biased toward the last-written role. The project reached basin-membership via the GHRR confound (066); the CL literature reaches the same NMC/prototype-distance readout via recency bias, two independent roads to do-not-use-top-1.
