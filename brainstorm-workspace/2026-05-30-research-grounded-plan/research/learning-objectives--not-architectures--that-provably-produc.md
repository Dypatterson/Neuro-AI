## Key findings
- (IN-CORPUS) Dorrell-Whittington 'Range, not Independence' (opened HTML v4, theorem+loss verbatim): objective is min over W_in,b_in,W_out,b_out of <||z||^2>_p(s) + lambda(||W_out||_F^2 + ||W_in||_F^2) s.t. z = W_in s + b_in, s = W_out z + b_out, z >= 0. CRITICAL: Theorem 2.1/2.2 modularity condition does NOT depend on lambda — it is determined purely by support geometry (an ellipse from extremal source values must fit inside the convex hull of the data). Nonnegativity z>=0 is ESSENTIAL (without it the principle disappears); the activity/energy penalty is NOT the driver. This sharpens/partially-corrects the diagnosis rank-3 framing: the activity-L2 magnitude is a red herring; load-bearing ingredients are (a) nonnegativity and (b) rectangular/spread support of the (role,filler) joint. Project built the sampler (068) but DEFERRED this exact training step; Report 111 substituted a store-state proxy and got 'novelty without retrieval'.  <https://arxiv.org/html/2410.06232v4>
- (NEW) DreamWeaver (ICLR 2025, opened HTML): discovers hierarchical compositional concepts from VIDEO unsupervised via a Recurrent Block-Slot Unit. Two anti-homunculus-clean mechanisms: (1) each block retrieves its state by ATTRACTOR DYNAMICS over a learned prototype library (implicit clustering by settling, not an arbiter) — analogous to Modern Hopfield basin retrieval; (2) a PREDICTIVE multi-frame objective L=(1/KL')sum CrossEntropy(z_{T+k,l}, o_{T->T+k,l}) instead of present-frame reconstruction. Predictive pressure forces factors that DON'T help present reconstruction (but DO help the future) to claim their own block. Direct answer to the project's 'centroid-mean, no margin' flat-write: a store-as-is target writes the mean; a PREDICTIVE target writes role-discriminative structure because the role selects which future follows.  <https://arxiv.org/html/2501.14174v1>
- (NEW) COMET 'Unsupervised Learning of Compositional Energy Concepts' (opened abstract; PDF binary-garbled, so abstract-depth ONLY): each factor is a SEPARATE energy function E_k(x,z_k); image is generated/reconstructed by OPTIMIZATION (gradient/Langevin) on the SUM of per-factor energies; factorization emerges with no supervision because 'recomposing the input captures independent factors'. Anti-homunculus shape excellent: no controller picks factors — the apparent decision lives in the joint energy-minimization. Cleanest template for porting to FHRR+Modern-Hopfield (already an energy model): per-role energy term, sum, settle. CAUTION: abstract-depth only; not load-bearing until carded from HTML/code at energy-based-model.github.io/comet.  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.03042>
- (NEW) Probabilistic Slot Attention identifiability (NeurIPS 2024, opened HTML): proves slots identifiable WITHOUT supervision up to permutation+affine (Prop 2) using ONLY a per-datapoint Gaussian-Mixture prior + reconstruction (NO contrastive term, NO negatives). Inductive bias forcing one-factor-per-slot is EM responsibility-assignment (implicit clustering) + automatic-relevance-determination pruning unused slots (pi_k->0). Relevance: a GENERATIVE mixture prior over role-slots can suffice for identifiability — alternative to the contrastive route (diagnosis rank-1) that sidesteps constructing role-shuffled negatives (the diagnosis's flagged FHRR-incompatibility risk). Settling-to-a-mixture-component is anti-homunculus-clean.  <https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07141v1>
- (NEW) Disentangled Representation Learning via Modular Compositional Bias (2025, opened PDF): independently corroborates Dorrell-Whittington from vision — 'disentanglement emerges from the SUPPORT of recombinations, not from independence'. Pure DATA/training-signal intervention (no architecture change): recombine latents under factor-specific rules (global attributes = mutually-exclusive swap; objects = additive/shared-support) + prior loss (every remix decodes realistically) + COMPOSITIONAL CONSISTENCY loss (composite-image latents must match the recombined latent vector). This is the mechanism-level version of the diagnosis rank-1 'role-shuffled negatives' AND rank-3 'rectangular support' unified: the recombination rule IS the rectangular-support condition; the consistency loss is the write-then-read forcing the role axis recoverable.  <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.21402>
- (NEW) Multimodal contrastive identifiability (ICLR 2023, abstract-depth ONLY): proves contrastive learning BLOCK-IDENTIFIES latent factors SHARED across two views/modalities up to a block-wise transform, even under nontrivial dependencies. Relevance: theoretical guarantee that InfoNCE-style alignment of two views sharing a role (two bindings, same role, different fillers) recovers the role subspace — literature SUPPORTS the diagnosis rank-1 contrastive route at block-identifiability level. CAUTION: 'block-identified' is WEAKER than 'disentangled/modular' (recovers the shared subspace as a block, not axis-aligned roles). Abstract-depth only; not load-bearing until carded.  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.09166>

## Concrete ideas

- **PREDICTIVE consolidation write (DreamWeaver port): replace the phase4 centroid-mean write with a self-supervised predictive target. For each stored binding (role r, filler f), the consolidation target is NOT 'store f at centroid' but 'from a partial role-cue r, predict the OTHER co-occurring filler in the same window' (cue-derived target via FHRR unbind-then-rebind, EMA/stop-grad on the target side). The role acquires discriminative structure because it is the only thing that selects which co-filler follows. This is the diagnosis rank-2 (JEPA) idea sharpened by DreamWeaver's finding that PREDICTION (not present-reconstruction) is the active ingredient.** [local-dynamic; Pre-Phase-5' Stage-1 toy (legitimately pre-fence per diagnosis; do NOT target the paused Phase 5' deltaE headline).]
  - writes structure: A reconstruction/store-as-is target is minimized by the members-mean (flat, EBM-2006 zero-margin). A predictive target r -> co-filler is minimized ONLY if the representation keeps whatever distinguishes the futures — the role axis. Hebbian/gradient pressure toward predicting distinct co-fillers from a shared role-cue writes a role-discriminative basin, supplying the margin the centroid-mean lacks.
  - test: Stage-1 write-then-read toy, ~4 roles, small codebook. Consolidate under (a) centroid-mean [control, must stay flat], (b) predictive cue->co-filler with EMA target. NATIVE readout: basin-membership top_index_hits for role-target above chance (NOT top-1, per Report 066). Falsifier: shuffled role-filler pairing must destroy recoverability. Add lower-D arm to rule out the capacity wall (058/065).

- **ACTUALLY run the Dorrell-Whittington nonneg+rectangular-support training objective the project DEFERRED. Report 068 built the rectangular sampler; Report 111 ran store-state proxies, NOT the objective. Run the real thing: a nonnegative-latent autoencoder z>=0 over the rectangular-support (role,filler) replay, loss = reconstruction + lambda*(||W||_F^2). Per the theorem, modularity is driven by rectangular support + nonnegativity, NOT lambda — so test lambda's irrelevance as a built-in control.** [offline-statistic; Phase 4 consolidation objective (the DEFERRED half of Report 068). Run as Stage-1 toy first.]
  - writes structure: Theorem 2.1/2.2: with nonnegative latent and spread (rectangular) support over (role,filler), the optimal autoencoder is FORCED to allocate disjoint neuron groups to role vs filler (an ellipse from extremal values fits inside the convex hull of the rectangular data). The write is the autoencoder weights; role-discriminative structure is the geometric consequence of nonnegativity + support, not an added margin term.
  - test: Stage-1 toy on Report 068 rectangular sampler output. Train nonneg AE (z>=0 via ReLU or a nonneg recoding layer since FHRR is signed/unit-magnitude — this recoding is the core port risk). Readout: linear probe role vs filler on z + basin-membership. Controls: (i) NON-rectangular (natural-skew) support must FAIL to modularize [theorem's falsifier]; (ii) sweep lambda 2 orders of magnitude — modularity must be invariant [confirms you reproduced the theorem, not an activity-penalty artifact]; (iii) lower-D arm.

- **COMET-style per-role ENERGY-SUM consolidation: give each role its own scalar energy term E_r(query, z_r) and define the consolidated readout as gradient/Langevin descent on SUM_r E_r. The Modern Hopfield substrate is already an energy model, so each role-energy is a native Hopfield-style term over that role's slot. No supervisor assigns fillers to roles — the descent settles it.** [local-dynamic; Phase 5' bundle-first / energy-write design (NOTE: targeting Phase 5' deltaE requires lifting the fence; the toy does not).]
  - writes structure: Each per-role energy is shaped by reconstruction-via-recomposition: the only way the summed energy reconstructs the bound query is if each E_r captures one factor (role's filler). Factorization is the fixed point of the joint descent, written into the per-role energy landscapes. Replaces the flat centroid with a sum of role-local basins.
  - test: Stage-1 toy: 2-4 per-role Hopfield energy terms, settle by descent on the sum, train terms so the settled state reconstructs held-out bindings. Readout: per-role basin-membership above chance. Control: collapse to ONE shared energy (no per-role split) must fail to separate roles; random-codebook must fail. CARD COMET first (currently abstract-depth only).

- **COMPOSITIONAL-CONSISTENCY write (Modular Compositional Bias port): add a consistency loss to consolidation — take two stored bindings sharing a role, SWAP fillers to make a recombined binding, require the recombined binding's encoded latent to MATCH the algebraically-recombined latent (unbind r, rebind f'); and decode realistically. This is the precise FHRR-native version of 'role-shuffled negatives' the diagnosis rank-1 wanted but couldn't specify; the swap is the recombination rule, the consistency loss is the write-then-read.** [offline-statistic; Phase 4 consolidation objective; run as Stage-1 toy.]
  - writes structure: If the role axis is NOT recoverable, swapping fillers under a fixed role produces a latent that does not match the algebraically-recombined latent — the consistency loss is nonzero and pushes the representation to make the role axis recoverable. Disentanglement emerges from the SUPPORT of valid recombinations (Dorrell corroboration), not from independence or an explicit margin.
  - test: Stage-1 toy: enumerate role-preserving filler swaps, minimize ||encode(swap) - rebind(r, f')||. Readout: basin-membership for role-target. Controls: (i) swap that also changes the role must NOT be required to match [asymmetry check]; (ii) random non-structured swaps must fail to modularize; (iii) lower-D arm. Offline/batch over the replay buffer, so anti-homunculus-exempt.

- **GENERATIVE-MIXTURE-PRIOR write (Probabilistic Slot Attention port): instead of contrastive negatives, put a von-Mises-mixture prior over the role-slots and consolidate by EM-style responsibility clustering + reconstruction. Each role-slot settles onto its mixture component; unused roles self-prune (pi_k->0). Sidesteps the hard 'construct role-shuffled negatives in complex-phasor FHRR space' problem the diagnosis flags as the live risk of rank-1.** [local-dynamic; Pre-Phase-5' Stage-1 toy.]
  - writes structure: EM responsibility assignment is implicit clustering: each role-slot's posterior concentrates on one filler-distribution, and reconstruction forces the slots to cover distinct factors. Identifiability (perm+affine, Prop 2) means the role partition is recovered up to relabeling. The write is the per-slot mixture parameters; role structure is the clustering fixed point.
  - test: Stage-1 toy: K role-slots, fit a per-window mixture by EM, reconstruct the binding. Readout: do slots align with ground-truth roles (basin-membership / ARI)? Control: single-component (K=1) must fail; shuffled fillers must fail; lower-D arm. FHRR is on the complex torus so use a von-Mises (not Gaussian) mixture — that adaptation is the port risk.

## Surprises
- SURPRISE 1 (challenges diagnosis rank-3 framing): Dorrell-Whittington's modularity is PROVABLY INDEPENDENT of the energy/activity-penalty weight lambda (verified at opened HTML, Thm 2.1/2.2: 'these inequalities do not depend on the energy trade-off parameter lambda'). The diagnosis proposes 'nonneg + reconstruction + activity-L2 separation pressure' as rank-3 — but the activity-L2 MAGNITUDE is a red herring per the actual theorem. The two load-bearing ingredients are NONNEGATIVITY and RECTANGULAR/spread SUPPORT.
- SURPRISE 2 (challenges 'range lever closed' status): Reports 068 and 111, read at primary, show the project NEVER ran the Dorrell nonneg-latent training objective. 068 built the sampler and DEFERRED training; 111 ran store-pre/post-settle-state PROXIES ('novelty without retrieval'). The objective the theorem is about — a nonnegative autoencoder on rectangular-support data — has not been executed. 'Range lever closed' is premature and may be a stale-status walk-back candidate.
- SURPRISE 3 (challenges diagnosis rank-1 'must construct negatives' assumption): Probabilistic Slot Attention (NeurIPS 2024) achieves UNSUPERVISED identifiability with NO contrastive negatives — just a mixture prior + EM clustering + reconstruction. The diagnosis treats 'construct the most-offending role-shuffled negative in complex-phasor FHRR space' as the central live risk of rank-1; the generative-mixture route sidesteps it entirely. A negatives-free path to role-discriminative structure may exist.
- SURPRISE 4 (convergent evidence strengthens diagnosis core): THREE independent communities (bio-autoencoders/Dorrell, vision-disentanglement/Modular-Compositional-Bias, object-centric/slot-attention) all conclude the SUPPORT/RANGE of factor combinations — not independence, not a margin added to a mean — is the active ingredient. This corroborates the diagnosis's 'flat/absence, not overlap' finding: a store-as-is centroid write never exposes the model to the spread of role-filler recombinations, so it CANNOT modularize regardless of margin tuning. The fix is the WRITE'S DATA/TARGET (recombination support + predictive/consistency target), which the diagnosis's surgical-in-place lean already favors.
