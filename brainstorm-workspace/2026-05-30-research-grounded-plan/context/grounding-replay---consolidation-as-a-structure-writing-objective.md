## Summary
Grounding 'Replay & consolidation as a STRUCTURE-WRITING objective'. I opened nine primaries (Dorrell-Whittington range-modularity, adaptation-replay Nature Comms, Bakermans compositional replay, Mattar prioritized replay, Aitchison recall-gating via web; Benna-Fusi, replay-review, MIR, InfoRS via local PDF) plus project primaries (Reports 016, 040, 062, 063, 068, 111 and the Phase-5 design headline). KEY FINDING: the headline lead (2410.06232) does NOT write structure from data alone — modularity EMERGES FROM OPTIMIZING a nonnegative + energy-efficient autoencoder loss to its minimum, and holds iff the source support is rectangular/range-spread. This localizes our failure: Report 068 built the rectangular-support SAMPLER on real traces but the REQUIRED training step (consolidate under a nonneg+energy objective on that support) was explicitly deferred and never run; our actual objective is Hebbian co-occurrence (Report 016) with no nonneg/energy/role term. So the one corpus mechanism that provably writes role/content modularity was never instantiated. Verified at primary that our nulls are FLAT/ABSENCE (hit_role=0.000 exactly across four families; role_score noise-dominated; sharpening makes it worse, Reports 062/063) — the role target sits where a random atom sits because no objective wrote it. I separate replay-that-PROTECTS (Benna-Fusi multi-timescale, freq-alpha already NULL in Report 040; DL catastrophic-forgetting replay) from replay-that-WRITES (range-modularity training; Bakermans factor-binding). The selection family (Mattar Gain x Need, MIR, InfoRS) FAILS the anti-homunculus filter as runtime metric-selectors; adaptation-replay PASSES as a local-dynamic generator but only traverses an existing landscape; recall-gating is thermostat-borderline and must be recast as a fixed local-state write-probability. Top candidate for Stage-1 write-then-read: the never-run nonneg+energy consolidation objective on rectangularized replay, with FHRR-nonnegativity-transfer as the central open risk. STATUS conflicts surfaced: Report 111's 'range-replay closed' is a storage variant, NOT the training-objective variant; Phase 5-prime fence must be lifted by the user before work re-enters it.

- **pdf:replay-review-2021** (primary_opened=True): The replay-in-deep-learning corpus is overwhelmingly protection-oriented: the canonical use is combating catastrophic forgetting under the stability-plasticity dilemma (preserve prior knowledge), not writing new relational geometry. The review notes biological replay has selectivity/partiality/sleep-phase elements missing from DL replay.
  - _caution:_ Read from local PDF; the 'missing biological elements' are descriptive, not an operationalized structure-writing recipe — do not over-read them as a mechanism.

- **pmc:PMC6203620, pdf:mir-2019, pdf:infors-2022** (primary_opened=True): The prioritized/interference/surprise replay-SELECTION family FAILS the anti-homunculus filter as a runtime mechanism. MIR selects samples by foreseen loss-increase under the next update. InfoRS computes per-point surprise+learnability and greedily picks.
  - _caution:_ These are SELECTION (which-to-replay) mechanisms, orthogonal to structure-WRITING; usable at most as precommitted offline replay weights, never runtime argmax.

- **doi:10.1038/s41467-025-68042-3** (primary_opened=True): Adaptation-driven replay (Nature Comms 2026, 'Dynamical modulation of hippocampal replay through firing rate adaptation') is the cleanest anti-homunculus-passing replay GENERATOR: a single local firing-rate-adaptation variable V (slow negative feedback, strength m, Eq.10) destabilizes a continuous-attractor bump and produces stationary/Brownian-diffusive/superdiffusive (forward/reverse) replay from ONE parameter, no controller selecting the mode. But it traverses a pre-existing FIXED attractor landscape and does NOT modify weights during replay.
  - _caution:_ link_only; confirmed via PMC mirror + search. It is a generator/readout, NOT a structure-writer — does not address hit_role=0.000 on its own.

- **pmc:PMC12081289** (primary_opened=True): Bakermans et al. give the mechanism by which replay CAN write compositional/factorized structure — but only when the representation is ALREADY factorized into independent sub-blocks (z=(z1,z2,...), e.g. grid cells x object-vector cells). Replay then binds the outer product of pre-existing factors into a hippocampal conjunction (a single visit suffices; multiple replays average path-integration noise). Replay composes factors; it does not create the factorization.
  - _caution:_ link_only; opened web primary. The precondition (pre-existing factorization) is met by FHRR positions x codebook, but 'conjunction written as a retrievable basin' is the untested transfer.

- **pmc:PMC11257680** (primary_opened=True): Recall-gated consolidation (Aitchison/PMC11257680) is a LOCAL gate that filters reliable from spurious memories: a short-term-memory module's recall strength rSTM = wSTM . w* gates whether long-term plasticity occurs (g(rSTM)); familiarity = recurrent-input correlation, no external oracle; it improves SNR of consolidated memories.
  - _caution:_ link_only; opened web primary. As published it is thermostat-shaped (reads recall, gates plasticity); FAILS the filter unless recast as a fixed local-state write-probability.

## Mechanisms for writing role structure

### Range/rectangular-support training under a nonnegative + energy-efficient autoencoder objective (Dorrell-Whittington), run as the consolidation objective on rectangularized replay  [arxiv:2410.06232, pdf:replay-review-2021]
- AH-shape: offline-statistic — Clean: fixed batch-offline optimization, no runtime metric-reader or arbitration. Rectangular support is a precommitted property of the replay distribution; nonneg+energy are fixed penalties. CONTROL: random (non-rectangular) support must NOT modularize (theorem predicts failure) — a built-in falsifier. CAUTION: requires a nonneg+energy objective the project lacks, and our FHRR substrate is signed/holographic, not a nonnegative firing-rate autoencoder — the nonnegativity constraint is load-bearing and porting it is non-trivial.
- maturity: proven-in-paper
- how it writes: Replace the current Hebbian-co-occurrence consolidation objective with a batch-offline reconstruction loss + nonnegativity (z>=0) + activity-L2/weight-Frobenius energy penalty, trained to a minimum on replay whose (role,content) joint support is rectangularized (the Report-068 sampler already produces this on real traces). The theorem guarantees the learned units MODULARIZE along role and content axes at the optimum, writing role-discriminative structure as a property of the minimum — not a tag. The role axis becomes a separable code a native readout can recover.
- relevance: Directly targets the root cause: our objective (Hebbian co-occurrence / settled-synergy, Report 016) has no nonneg/energy/role term, so it never writes a role->target minimum (hit_role=0.000). Report 068 already built the rectangular-support half; the never-run half is exactly this training objective. Highest-grounded candidate for the Stage-1 write-then-read go/no-go.

### Replay-binds-pre-existing-factors into role->target basins (Bakermans compositional replay), with a binding-as-basin consolidation step  [pmc:PMC12081289]
- AH-shape: offline-statistic — Clean IF the replay distribution and write rule are precommitted offline (sampling weights are fixed functions of buffer marginals — Report 068 already passed this check). No runtime selection. CONTROL: shuffled role-atom pairing must destroy recoverability. CAUTION: close to what Report 111 tried ('novelty without retrieval'); the difference that matters is writing the conjunction as a RETRIEVABLE Hopfield basin (store the bound vector) vs storing a post-settle state — Report 111 did the latter.
- maturity: plausible
- how it writes: Given role and content as already-independent FHRR factors (position vectors x content codebook — which we have), replay synthesizes recombined (role, content) conjunctions (Report-068 rebind-on-the-fly: bind(positions[role], codebook[atom])) and the consolidation step writes each conjunction as a stored pattern/basin via the outer-product/Hebbian write. A single visit per (role,content) cell suffices; repeated replay averages noise. Structure is written because the basin IS the role->target conjunction, recoverable by unbind-then-cleanup.
- relevance: Our role/content ARE already factorized (FHRR positions x codebook), so the Bakermans precondition is met. The gap is a consolidation step that writes the recombined conjunction as a basin rather than co-occurrence statistics. Cleanly distinguished from the closed Report-111 storage variant.

### Recall-gated batch consolidation as an SNR filter on which conjunctions get written (Aitchison), reformulated as a fixed local-state write-probability  [pmc:PMC11257680]
- AH-shape: thermostat-risk — BORDERLINE. As published it is a gate g(rSTM) reading recall and gating plasticity — thermostat-shaped. PASSES only if recast as a fixed precommitted local-state function (write-probability = smooth function of familiarity, applied uniformly offline) with NO outcome-inspecting branch. CONTROL: ungated batch consolidation must show lower role-channel SNR. If the if-recall-then-write branch cannot be removed, it FAILS the filter.
- maturity: plausible
- how it writes: During offline consolidation, the probability that a (role,content) conjunction is written to long-term store is a FIXED function of the substrate's own recall strength for that conjunction (familiarity = recurrent-input correlation). Reliable role->target structure (consistently recallable) accumulates; transient/noise conjunctions are filtered out, raising the SNR of the role channel above the FHRR-unbinding noise floor that currently buries it (Report 063 noise-dominated role_score).
- relevance: Our role_score is noise-dominated (Report 063): the role-target is not separable from noise-correlated atoms. An SNR-raising filter that preferentially consolidates reliably-recurring role->target conjunctions is the most direct attack on the noise floor — but only if it survives the anti-homunculus recast.

### Adaptation-driven replay as a structure-AGNOSTIC generator (Nature Comms 2026) to FEED a structure-writing objective, not to write structure itself  [doi:10.1038/s41467-025-68042-3, pdf:replay-review-2021]
- AH-shape: local-dynamic — Cleanest local-dynamic example in the cluster: forward/reverse/diffusive modes all emerge from one fixed adaptation strength m via local settling, no metric-reader, no mode-selector. But weights are FIXED during replay — it reads out, it does not write. Treat as a generator, never as the structure-writer.
- maturity: proven-in-paper
- how it writes: Does NOT write structure on its own: a single local adaptation variable destabilizes an attractor bump and generates forward/reverse/diffusive replay sequences that TRAVERSE the existing landscape. Its value is as an anti-homunculus-clean replay GENERATOR (one parameter, no mode-selector) that could supply the trajectory stream a separate structure-writing objective (mechanism 1 or 2) consolidates.
- relevance: Tangential to the write-structure question but directly relevant to the anti-homunculus constraint: if a future design needs a replay scheduler, this shows how to get diverse forward/reverse replay from a local dynamic instead of a Mattar-style Gain x Need thermostat. Does not address hit_role=0.000 by itself.

## Cautions
- The headline lead (2410.06232), adaptation-replay (doi:10.1038/s41467-025-68042-3), Bakermans (PMC12081289), Mattar (PMC6203620), recall-gating (PMC11257680) are all status=link_only. I OPENED each primary this run (web), but no card exists yet — write cards before any grounds a build. Benna-Fusi, replay-review, MIR, InfoRS are stale_pdf, read from local PDF.
- Dorrell-Whittington's nonnegativity (z>=0) is load-bearing in the theorem. Our substrate is FHRR (signed, holographic, unit-magnitude), not a nonnegative firing-rate autoencoder. Porting the modularity guarantee to FHRR is non-trivial and unproven — may require a nonnegative re-coding layer. This is the central open risk before committing to mechanism 1.
- 'Literature supports' vs 'this project has shown' must stay separate: the literature SUPPORTS that a nonneg energy-efficient autoencoder trained on rectangular support modularizes (2410.06232, proven-in-paper). This project has SHOWN only (a) the SAMPLER rectangularizes real traces (Report 068, n=3 spike) and (b) a DIFFERENT storage-side range variant fails downstream (Report 111). The training-objective variant that writes role structure is the never-run experiment.
- Report 111's closure ('novelty without retrieval') is of a post-settle storage variant, NOT the Dorrell-Whittington training objective. Do not let STATUS.md's 'range-shaped replay downstream lane closed' be read as closing the structure-writing-objective question. Surface this distinction to the user before any range-shaped work is dismissed.
- The selection-family papers (Mattar Gain x Need, MIR foreseen-interference, InfoRS surprise+learnability) are runtime metric-reader/selectors and FAIL the anti-homunculus filter as written. Usable ONLY reframed as fixed offline/batch statistics (precommitted replay weights computed once over the buffer, not recomputed-and-argmaxed per step). Mattar's authors concede the Gain term is 'admittedly unrealistic' online.
- Recall-gated plasticity (mechanism 3) is thermostat-shaped as published. Recast as a fixed local-state write-probability with no outcome-inspecting branch, or it fails the filter. Do not adopt the if-recall>theta-then-consolidate form.
- Phase 5-prime is PAUSED per STATUS.md; the Phase 5 graduation headline is dE = E_content-prior - E_role-prior (CI>0, floor 5.5e-3, phase-5-unified-design.md:282-297). Any structure-writing mechanism here would be tested under the active branch's Stage-1 write-then-read toy (kickoff seed), NOT inside paused Phase 5-prime. The user must explicitly lift the Phase 5-prime fence before work re-enters it.
