### Confounds / alternative readings
- EVAL DESIGN confound: the 062/063/064 smokes initialize branches DIRECTLY from the role-target / content-distractor / random atoms, bypassing the schema-store selector and log-prior machinery (062:102). This is the MOST-favorable-possible cue construction (you start AT the target), which strengthens the null for D1/D3 but means the smokes do not test the full Phase 5 pipeline; a different cue construction with schema-store priors 'might surface a different signal' (062:102, author's own caveat).
- READOUT confound: hit_role is argmax-over-1064-atoms (top-1) and rank_role is similarity rank. These are FHRR-cosine readouts on settled state, NOT Hopfield-basin membership. 066 is the cautionary precedent: top1 looked like 100% rescue but top_index_hits (true basin rate) was 0/3072. The 'flat absence' rests on top-1/rank readouts that 066 itself proved can disagree with basin retrieval by ~95 points. The standing project caution 'algebraic/decode success != Hopfield basin retrieval' cuts BOTH ways here.
- CAPACITY-WALL confound: 065 shows key-only retrieval is above chance at low N and decays to chance with N; the role-binding null could be partly a capacity-saturation / 1-over-sqrt-D crosstalk-floor effect at D=4096 with 1024-1064 atoms, rather than 'structure never written'. 058:156 leaves the lower-D question explicitly open. If it is capacity, the fix is dimension/orthogonalization (the seed's own 'exchange-rate not impossibility' framing at lines 50-52), not the consolidation objective.
- METRIC-ARTIFACT confound on the EXACT 0.000: hit_role=0.000 EXACTLY across hundreds of cues is consistent with 'role-target is essentially never top-1 out of ~1064', which is also what a near-uniform/diffuse readout produces. The exact zero is evidence of ABSENCE-OF-TOP-1-HIT, which is slightly weaker than ABSENCE-OF-WRITTEN-STRUCTURE: structure written at rank 50-200 (well above chance but never top-1) would still read hit_role=0.000. rank_role (~205-540, i.e. mid-pack, near the 532 random-midpoint) is what actually rules out 'written but not top-1' — and rank_role is the load-bearing number, not the headline-grabbing 0.000.
- ELIMINATION-INFERENCE confound: the conclusion 'the learning/consolidation step is the culprit' is reached by ELIMINATING retrieval-side interventions (D1/D3/E1/M1 all null), per the Bayesian 'shared upstream component' argument (064:81). This is sound but indirect: no experiment positively shows a DIFFERENT consolidation objective writes recoverable role basins. The shared upstream component is the substrate; whether the defect is in the consolidation OBJECTIVE specifically vs the binding ALGEBRA (066's deeper 'may not have key-only basins for ANY binding operator', 066:45) vs the encoding/capacity is not disambiguated by these reports.
- SEED-INTERNAL DERIVATION (not a report cite, so not a fabrication, but flag for accuracy): the seed's 'Random-middle is ~532' (seed line 38) is the 1064/2 midpoint, a derived figure, NOT a quoted value from 058. ### Confounds / alternative readings
- CAPACITY-WALL confound (the strongest alternative reading): at L=512 the lift went NEGATIVE (-0.006), meaning the Hopfield landscape exceeded capacity at beta=10 and the consolidation SIGNAL collapsed. The sigma_A(512)=0.139 was measured in a regime where the mechanism is broken, not operating. So 'sigma_A doesn't fall with L' is partly confounded by 'L=512 is past the capacity cliff.' The clean evidence for irreducibility is really the L=64->256 segment (sigma_A 0.157->0.150, only ~4.5% drop while the lift was still healthy and rising to +0.050) — a 2-point trend. The precommit's own VARIANCE-IRREDUCIBLE threshold (sigma_A(512)>=0.13) is read at the broken operating point.
- METRIC ARTIFACT / contrast-pairing confound: Report 118 §5 (lines 89-92) raises that consolidation amplifying variance may be the system CORRECTLY adapting the codebook to each seed's landscape draw, in which case the real-shuffle DiD is simply the WRONG CONTRAST (it pairs worlds on the codebook, not on the landscape; corr_AC_BD=0.10 shows that pairing buys nothing). Under this reading the 'irreducible variance' is an estimand-design artifact, not a property that dooms the underlying mechanism — a landscape-controlled contrast (pairing seeds on the same landscape draw) might cancel it. This directly weakens 'the learning step is the culprit.'
- READOUT confound (cross-line, from the project framing): Phase 5 found hit_role=0.000 across four retrieval families and the GSBC kickoff concluded role structure was never WRITTEN. If the substrate's native readout (Recall@K via the existing FHRR/MHN path) is itself lossy/insensitive in a way that inflates seed-to-seed variance, the 'consolidation injects variance' signature could partly be a readout-instability signature. The reports do not isolate readout noise from consolidation-dynamics noise; sigma_C (frozen) being non-zero (0.069) shows the readout+landscape draw alone carries appreciable variance.
- SINGLE-RUN / no-CI confound: every sigma claim is a point estimate over one n=10 run (L=64 is the recovered run; L=256/512 is one parallel run). The ~96/4 window-vs-atom variance split is explicitly flagged in Report 115 §2 (lines 69-78) as a provenance-flagged, off-repo, single-df=9 ranking where only the ~24x ordinal margin survives the noise — NOT a reproducible percentage. Treating any of these SDs as stable population values overstates precision.
- EVAL-DESIGN confound: window_seed_override re-runs the entire reseed chain (landscape draw + consolidation corpus), so each 'window-average' draw is a fresh codebook. The interpretation that variance is consolidation-intrinsic rests on the A-vs-C (consolidated vs frozen, SAME landscape per seed) comparison being clean. If the frozen arm C does not see byte-identical consolidation-corpus exposure per seed, some of the A-C gap could be exposure-asymmetry rather than consolidation dynamics. The reports assert C is the within-cell baseline but I did not re-open the c3 reseed code to confirm A and C share the identical landscape per seed beyond the report's own audit (Report 117 §1 lines 23-32).
