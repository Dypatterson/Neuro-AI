## Summary
Target geometry: role-discriminative structure should look like a simplex ETF -- equinorm, maximally-equiangular role basins (Papyan neural collapse, primary opened) -- which at D=4096 is dimensionally feasible. But neural collapse gets that geometry from SUPERVISED labels; the project's unsupervised co-occurrence consolidation has no role label, so the ETF mechanism does not transfer for free. The cluster's three candidate UNSUPERVISED writing pressures are: (1) a free-energy consolidation objective with an explicit model-complexity/compression term (2505.22749, primary opened) -- the strongest, anti-homunculus-clean, because the compression penalty supplies the 'separate basins' force labels otherwise give, and a no-complexity ablation is both the control and the explanation for Reports 117/118's irreducible consolidation-injected variance (no force pulls clusters into the tight/collapsed regime); (2) a slowness/decorrelation objective (SFA, PMC1963505, primary opened) that writes role onto slow decorrelated axes -- plausible but its orthogonality is an IMPOSED batch-eigenvalue constraint, exempt only as an offline pass; (3) an inhibitory NREM-style differentiate-and-stabilize pass (Tucker/Luu/Friston pubmed:40422982, primary opened, but conceptual/abstract-only, lowest maturity). Geometry of Consolidation (primary opened) gives the diagnostic law (eps_id, tight vs spread regime by d_bar/theta'/d_eff) that most likely explains OUR failure: the project has never measured where its consolidated codebook sits on that regime diagram, and the hypothesis that consolidation keeps it in the spread regime (hence hit_role=0 and irreducible sigma_A) is testable cheaply with the in-repo d_eff/cap-coverage instrument but is currently UNVERIFIED. Literature SUPPORTS that a complexity-penalized / slowness / inhibitory offline consolidation objective could write separable role basins; the project has SHOWN only the negative -- hit_role=0.000 flat across four retrieval families and consolidation-injected, landscape-independent variance. The honest next step is a measurement (place the codebook on the consolidation-law regime diagram) plus a toy write-then-read of the compression-penalty objective, gated behind a full card for 2505.22749 and the user lifting the Phase 5-prime fence."

## Mechanisms for writing role structure

### Free-energy / predictive-coding consolidation with an explicit complexity (compression) penalty (self-orthogonalizing attractors)  [arxiv:2505.22749, pdf:neural-collapse-2020]
- AH-shape: local-dynamic — (a) Quantity that moves locally: coupling weights, via prediction-error x activity. (b) Apparent decision (separate vs overlap basins) lives in the energy/learning dynamic minimising accuracy+complexity, not in any reader. (c) The fixed dynamic replacing 'if role then write' is gradient descent on a single free-energy functional applied every pass. (d) Control: a no-complexity-term ablation should FAIL to separate (recover hit_role~0), isolating the compression penalty as the cause -- and this doubles as the falsification. CAVEAT: must be a batch-offline pass; online error-driven codebook updates are BANNED at runtime per STATUS, and the 2505.22749 equation details were paraphrased not verified.
- maturity: plausible
- how it writes: An OFFLINE/batch consolidation pass updates the Hopfield/codebook couplings by a predictive-coding rule (delta-coupling ~ prediction-error x activity) under a free-energy objective = accuracy + model-complexity. The accuracy term stores patterns; the complexity/compression term makes overlapping basins expensive, so minimising it WRITES approximately orthogonalized attractor basins into the coupling matrix. This is the unsupervised analogue of how labels drive neural-collapse ETF: the compression penalty supplies the 'separate' pressure that labels otherwise provide. Role-discriminative targets become low-energy minima because keeping role and content in distinct basins is what minimises long-term surprise + description length.
- relevance: Directly targets the diagnosed root cause (the learning step never writes a role->target basin). It is the cluster's strongest candidate for an anti-homunculus-clean WRITING objective: it adds a separation pressure (complexity penalty) the current co-occurrence consolidation lacks, which is exactly why role-target currently sits where a random atom sits. It also predicts the Report 117/118 variance: a consolidation objective with NO compression term has no force pulling clusters into the tight/collapsed regime, so within-cluster spread (sigma_A) stays high and irreducible.

### Slowness / temporal-decorrelation consolidation objective (SFA-style offline pass)  [pmc:PMC1963505]
- AH-shape: offline-statistic — (a) Locally: feature weights move to reduce temporal derivative. (b) The 'decision' to separate features lives in the decorrelation constraint of the objective. (c) Fixed dynamic: minimise Delta under unit-variance + decorrelation, applied as a batch pass. (d) Control: shuffle the temporal order within windows (destroy slowness structure) -> separation should vanish. IMPORTANT: as published, SFA solves this as a GLOBAL eigenvalue problem with Lagrange-multiplier-imposed orthogonality -- that is an offline statistic / batch optimisation, NOT a runtime local dynamic, and the orthogonality is IMPOSED not emergent. It is exempt from the thermostat ban only as an offline pass; it must not become a runtime metric-reader.
- maturity: plausible
- how it writes: An offline consolidation pass over replayed trajectories minimises temporal change of role-coding features subject to a decorrelation constraint. Because role identity is approximately constant across a window while content varies, the slowness objective writes role onto the slow, mutually-decorrelated axes and content onto faster axes -- factorising the code so the role-target becomes recoverable on its own slow subspace. Which factor lands on which axis is set by the input statistics (here: role changes slowly relative to content), not by a label.
- relevance: Gives a concrete, statistics-driven reason a consolidation objective WOULD write a role/content factorization without labels: role is the slow variable. The current Path-C co-occurrence consolidation has no slowness pressure, consistent with role structure never being laid down. Requires the project to actually have role-slow / content-fast temporal structure in replay -- a precondition to check, and overlaps with the range-shaped-replay lever STATUS already flags as partially closed (Report 111); re-read 111 before proposing.

### Inhibitory differentiate-and-stabilize offline pass (NREM-style anti-Hebbian consolidation)  [pubmed:40422982]
- AH-shape: offline-statistic — (a) Locally: couplings decrease between co-active-but-distinct representations (anti-Hebbian/LTD). (b) The 'separate' decision lives in the fixed LTD update sign, applied in an offline pass. (c) No if/then: the same depression rule applies to all co-active pairs. (d) Control: ablate the inhibitory pass (excitatory-only consolidation) -> expect the anti-overlap signature to persist and hit_role to stay 0. RISK: an inhibitory pass that READS which atoms to depress at runtime would be a thermostat -- it must be a fixed local LTD rule over replayed pairs, offline only.
- maturity: speculative
- how it writes: An offline (sleep-analog) consolidation pass applies an inhibitory / anti-Hebbian (long-term-depression-like) update that DIFFERENTIATES newly stored representations from existing ones before a separate excitatory pass integrates them. Mechanically this lowers the coupling between a new role-target basin and its current nearest competitors, pushing the role-target atom out of the crowded random-middle region (rank ~205-498) toward a separated basin -- writing discriminability by carving troughs between basins rather than only deepening wells.
- relevance: Maps onto the anti-overlap signature the project already sees (sharpening made it worse): if the substrate currently only deepens wells (Hebbian) without carving inter-basin troughs (inhibition), role-targets stay in the crowded middle. But the source is a conceptual neuroscience proposal with NO formalism, NO metric, and abstract-only access -- it is an inspiration for an inhibitory consolidation term, not an implementable recipe. Lowest maturity in the set.

### Compositional energy minimisation as the READ-side complement (composing learned per-factor energies)  [arxiv:2510.20607]
- AH-shape: local-dynamic — (a) Locally: state descends gradient of summed energy. (b) The 'decision' is which composite minimum is reached, set by energy summation, no router. (c) Fixed dynamic: E_total = E_role + E_content, minimise. (d) Control: compose energies of factors that were NOT cleanly separated during writing and show retrieval fails -- which is precisely the project's current state and would confirm the writing gap is upstream.
- maturity: speculative
- how it writes: Does NOT itself write per-factor structure during consolidation; it assumes per-factor (e.g. per-role, per-content) energy landscapes were learned separately, then at inference ADDITIVELY composes them and descends the summed energy. Its relevance to writing is indirect: it specifies the TARGET interface a writing objective must produce -- separable per-factor energies that sum without destructive interference -- which in turn tells the consolidation objective what 'role-discriminative structure' must look like to be composable.
- relevance: Frames the success criterion: the project's hit_role=0 is what you get when per-factor energies were never written separably, so composition has nothing to compose. Useful as a downstream read-side once a writing objective exists; not a writing mechanism on its own. Abstract-only; does not resolve the overlap-tolerance question. Pair with, do not substitute for, a writing objective.
