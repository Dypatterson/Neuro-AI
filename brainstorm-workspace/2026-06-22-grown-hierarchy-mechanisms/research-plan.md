emergent ones — vanilla deep nets, Report 132 depth — fail).

## Central question

What concrete mechanisms — in ML and in neuroscience — can **grow a compositional
abstraction hierarchy level-by-level**, with a **stabilization signal gating when each
new level forms**, and which are **buildable on the existing MCD/COGS harness**
(`experiments/87` / `94` / `99`) under Bet-B rules (backprop / global / multi-layer
allowed; one-shot SVD/eig still a flashlight; full discipline kept)?

The load-bearing distinction every candidate must be placed against: **GROWN/emergent**
(the open door) vs **INJECTED/enforced** (Tree-LSTM, NeSS — the field's winners = the
ceiling to approach, not the answer).

## Fan-out threads

**A — ML "grown hierarchy" mechanisms (primary, buildability-weighted).**
Progressive / growing nets (Net2Net, progressive networks, dynamically-expanding nets,
firefly/architecture growth); recursive / iterated-depth (Universal Transformers,
Adaptive Computation Time, looped/recurrent-depth transformers); latent-tree &
unsupervised grammar induction (ON-LSTM ordered-neurons, Gumbel-tree / soft-tree, DIORA,
URNNG); object-centric / slot hierarchies (slot attention stacks); hierarchical VAEs /
ladder networks; growing mixture-of-experts / modular routing that adds modules.
*For each: is the hierarchy grown or injected, and is the depth/level count emergent?*

**B — Neuroscience hierarchy-formation mechanisms (grounding the gating coupling).**
Cortical hierarchy & developmental staging; hierarchical predictive coding (PFC schema
over sensory detail); systems consolidation building schema hierarchies (Tse et al.
schema-gating; CLS; Lewis–Durrant sleep gist-extraction / replay-interleaving); grid /
place / successor-representation hierarchies and structural learning (TEM); synaptic
homeostasis (Tononi–Cirelli) as the gating *stabilizer*. *Target: does the biology
support "stabilize level N → enables level N+1," or only flat schema formation?*

**C — The stabilization→abstraction GATING coupling (the novelty, both fields).**
Mechanisms where stabilizing level N is a *precondition/trigger* for forming level N+1:
critical periods / maturational gating, self-paced & readiness-gated curricula,
progressive-freezing/distillation, emergence-thresholded growth. *Most of A/B build
hierarchy WITHOUT an explicit stabilization gate — isolate the few that couple them.*

**D — Operationalization & ceiling (the deliverable).**
For each surviving candidate: buildable on `87/94/99`? where on grown-vs-injected? what
is the decisive experiment shape (the control that makes a win mean "grown, not
injected")? Injected-hierarchy methods (Tree-LSTM, NeSS, grammar machines) covered
explicitly as the **comparison ceiling** — what accuracy a grown mechanism must approach
on the GECA-resistant MCD arena (Report 146) to matter.

## Discipline (inherit from the 06-21 recon)

- **Adversarial 3-vote per-claim verification**: every load-bearing claim gets a
  claim → strongest-disconfirmer → verdict pass; verify hype against primaries.
- **Grown-vs-injected is the arbiter**, not benchmark score: an injected-hierarchy win
  is REDUNDANT-WITH-KNOWN, not a reopen.
- **Leakage / contamination guard** on any "emergent win" claim (cf. Dong et al. 2024 in
  the prior recon): a held-out compositional win must not be a leak artifact.
- **Anti-homunculus check** on any proposed mechanism before it lands on the shortlist.
